A potion of Wind Charging is a potion that provides Wind Charged when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Breeze Rod + Awkward Potion → Potion of Wind Charging.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Wind Charging → Splash Potion of Wind Charging.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Wind Charging → Lingering Potion of Wind Charging.
