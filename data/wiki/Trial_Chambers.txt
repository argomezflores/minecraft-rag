TITLE: Trial Chambers
CATEGORY: Generated structures
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_Chambers
---
Trial chambers are uncommon underground structures that serve as a mid-game combat challenge. They consist mostly of copper blocks and tuff bricks, and are the only place where trial spawners and vaults naturally generate. Trial chambers are also the only place where breezes spawn, whose breeze rods can be used to craft wind charges. Various materials such as heavy cores and certain armor trims are obtained exclusively by unlocking vaults in trial chambers. As it is the only source of breeze rods and heavy cores, the structure is also the only location where maces can be obtained.

Trial chambers can also be used to obtain copper bulbs without entering the Nether, and large quantities of copper blocks and their derivatives.

Most copper blocks found in trial chambers are waxed, preventing them from oxidizing. However, some unwaxed copper blocks can also generate due to a bug.

Ominous trials can also take place here through the Bad Omen effect. Bad Omen converts to Trial Omen, which causes all trial spawners to become ominous, making them more difficult and providing greater rewards.

Generation
Trial chambers generate underground in the Overworld. The starting room generates at an altitude of between Y=-40 and -20, but the structure is not restricted to those Y levels; the majority of the structure's rooms tend to generate between Y=-20 and 0.

The generation of trial chambers follows a grid of 34×34 chunk regions centered on the world origin (X=0, Z=0). There is always one trial chambers structure generated in each region at a random location, unless a deep dark biome cancels the generation of the trial chambers.

While the origin point of the structure () cannot generate within a deep dark biome, other parts of it can intersect a deep dark biome.

Trial chambers are one of the only structures that generate on the same locations on Java Edition and Bedrock Edition.

Locating
Trial chambers can be located using buried trial chambers maps, which can be obtained in two ways.
Buried trial chambers maps are sold by journeyman-level cartographer villagers for 12 emeralds and a compass. The maps always point to the nearest trial chamber relative to the cartographer that does not already have a buried trial chambers map that points to it. All maps bought from the same cartographer always point to the same structure.
Buried trial chambers maps can be found in loot of secret chests in abandoned camps. (see ).

Trial chambers are buried underground, so exposed caves will usually have weird shapes that will determine the location of a trial chamber.

, a given villager always trades the same map, which is determined upon the trade unlocking.

Components
Corridors
thumb|A diagram depicting every piece that constitutes a corridor.

Trial chambers are composed of two corridors:
a corridor going from the entrance room to the intersection, passing through four slices and the atrium;
a corridor going from the intersection to the end room, passing through four slices.

The intersection connects both corridors, forming an L-shape. The intersection and the end room always lead to a chamber (see ), while slices have a probability to lead to a hallway (see ).

Entrance
thumb|The doorway found in all entrance rooms.

The entrance generates at the end of one of the two corridors, passing through the atrium. Each entrance contains a "doorway" that consists of waxed oxidized copper blocks, including its cut copper and chiseled copper variants.

entrance_1 and entrance_2 include a vault, two entrance chests, and a platform containing either an oak, a mangrove, or a cactus; additionally, entrance_1 has a secret room containing a bed and a chest.

entrance_3 includes two vaults and three entrance chests.

Slices
thumb|A door leading from a corridor to a hallway.
thumb|A corridor viewed from the ground.

Corridor slices, or straight pieces, are sub-structures that make up the corridors; they are generated on top of a tuff brick plate. Each of the two corridors has four slices, composed randomly.

Except for straight_1 and straight_8, all slices have a copper door:
straight_2 and straight_5 generate a copper door on the lower level;
straight_3 and straight_6 generate a copper door on the middle level;
straight_4 and straight_7 generate a copper door on the upper level.

On average, each trial chamber has 6.29 () doors. These copper doors can either lead directly to a chamber, a hallway or nothing. Provided the latter case doesn't happen:
lower level doors always lead to a descending spiral staircase ( chance between lower_hallway_connector or trapped_staircase);
upper level doors always lead to an ascending spiral staircase (upper_hallway_connector);
middle level doors and these spiral staircases have a 97.48% chance to directly lead to a chamber, 2.44% chance to lead to a hallway piece, and 0.08% chance to directly lead to a dead-end (see );
any doors in any level, especially the spiral staircases leading to them in the case of upper and lower level doors, can also generate dead-end pieces as fallback pieces.

Both straight_6 and straight_8 generate with a trial spawner that can either spawn a melee, small melee or ranged mob (see ), but always never breezes. straight_7 generates with a hidden vault.

Slices can generate with various decorations such as flower pots, decorated pots, candles and corridor barrels (see ). Slices can have addons, some of them generate with a trial spawner (that can either spawn a melee, small melee or ranged mob, but never breezes), a dispenser trap or a vault. Some slices cannot contain specific types of or all corridor addons.

Hallways

thumb|A hallway viewed from the ground.

Hallways are segments that connect corridors to chambers. When a copper door generates in a corridor, it (or, in the case of an upper or lower door, the spiral staircase that follows it; see ) may lead to one of the following pieces:
a chamber (97.48%);
a hallway segment (1.14%);
an encounter (0.41%);
a dead end (0.08%).

If the selected piece cannot be generated, the game attempts to generate a different piece. After a certain number of failed attempts, it defaults to generating a dead end. If the dead end itself cannot be generated, no piece is generated.

This process is then repeated at the end of each hallway segment or encounter. Furthermore, hallways never branch, meaning that every copper door found in corridors eventually leads to a single chamber, a dead end, or nothing.

Chambers

Chambers are large combat rooms that contain multiple trial spawners and vaults, as well as one ominous vault. Chambers can be accessed in numerous ways:
they have a chance to generate in corridor slices (chambers can generate straight from slices by middle level copper doors) and hallways, in this case, all chambers can generate;
they can always generate in intersections, also in this case, all chambers can generate;
additionally, , ,  and  can generate in the end of corridor ends.

Each chamber design has several potential entrances, of which only one is connected to the rest of the structure. The remaining entrances are sealed with an entrance cap (see ). Each potential entrance, including the sealed ones, generates a disposal consisting of a hopper placed above a barrel (see ), allowing players to manage their inventory before entering a chamber.

Mobs

Natural mob spawning do not occur within the trial chambers. Instead, mobs can spawn in the trial chambers through trial spawners or unnatural mob spawning sources.
Mobs from trial spawners

link=Breeze link=Bogged link=Cave Spider link=Husk link=Silverfish link=Skeleton link=Slime link=Spider link=Stray link=Zombie Breeze Bogged Cave Spider Husk Silverfish Skeleton Slime Spider Stray Zombie and baby zombie
These mobs above spawn from the trial spawners. The number of mobs spawned scales with the number of players in range of each spawner, and breezes are spawned less per spawner and per player than other mobs.

When a trial chamber generates, one mob from each of the following categories is randomly selected for the trial spawners in that trial chamber. Every mob within the same category has an equal chance to be selected. There are four (or five if the slow ranged category is counted) categories for trial chamber mob spawning.
Breeze (always spawn a breeze)
Melee ( chance to spawn either a husk, a spider or a zombie)
Ranged and slow ranged ( chance to spawn either a bogged, a skeleton or a stray)
Small melee ( chance to spawn either a baby zombie, a cave spider, a silverfish or a slime)
Not all trial chambers will spawn with all four categories. However, the breeze category is guaranteed to spawn in all trial chambers.

Loot

Loot is available from vaults, chests, barrels, dispensers, and decorated pots from within the chambers. Players who defeat a trial spawner each receive rewards; this loot scales depending on the number of players who defeat the trial spawner.

Trivia
The idea of trial chambers evolved from CornerHard pitching in a meeting to revisit the monster room.
The design of trial chambers was inspired by the architecture of n civilizations, whose geometric designs fit well into Minecraft. The design combines the aesthetic of ancient civilizations with a futuristic feel.
A scrapped chamber design appears in two YouTube videos showcasing the trial chambers and the trial spawners. It includes a trial spawner, two chests, and two redstone lamps with levers on top.
Brandon Pearce thinks that whoever created trial chambers in-universe seem to be "pretty tinkery".
There are around 4,500 copper blocks in an average trial chamber. If these were all mined and converted to copper ingots, this would be approximately 40,000 copper ingots.
