Concrete stairs are a craftable stair variant of concrete. They come in 16 colors.

Obtaining
Breaking
Concrete stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Matching Concrete, Matching Concrete, Matching Concrete, Matching Concrete, Matching Concrete, Matching Concrete arranged in the crafting grid → Matching Concrete Stairs,4.

Stonecutting

Usage

Concrete stairs come in sixteen colors, which are white, light gray, gray, black, brown, red, orange, yellow, lime, green, cyan, light blue, blue, purple, magenta, and pink.
