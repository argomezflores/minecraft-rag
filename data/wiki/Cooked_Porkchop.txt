A cooked porkchop is a food item obtained by cooking a raw porkchop. Cooked porkchops and steak restore the most hunger out of all stackable foods.

Obtaining

Mob loot

Generated loot

Cooking
Raw porkchop can be cooked in a furnace, smoker, or campfire. Each piece of cooked porkchop removed from a furnace output slot gives 0.35 experience (22.4 experience per stack).

Smelting recipe: Raw Porkchop → Cooked Porkchop, XP: 0,35.

Trading

Villager gifts
Butcher villagers may give cooked porkchop to players with the Hero of the Village effect.

Usage
Food

To eat cooked porkchop, press and hold  while it is selected in the hotbar. Eating one restores  hunger and 12.8 saturation.

Wolves
Cooked porkchops can be used to breed and heal tamed wolves, lead them around, and make baby tamed wolves grow up faster by 10% of the remaining time.

Piglins
Piglins pick up any cooked porkchops in their item form. However, they do not eat it.
