Achievements (known as trophies on PlayStation) are ways to gradually guide new players into Minecraft and give them rewarding challenges to complete, similar to the system of advancements .

There are 133 achievements or 136 trophies available.

Obtaining
Every achievement is tracked per user account in Minecrafts social system. They are not tracked separately per world; achievements earned in one world apply to all worlds using that edition and that user account. Achievements are tracked separately on each platform of Bedrock Edition; they do not carry over to other platforms when using the same account. On most platforms, profile data including achievements is logged to a Microsoft account, so players must be logged in to their Microsoft account to earn and see them. On PlayStation, achievements are logged as trophies to the player's console account, and if logged into a PlayStation Network account and online, they are synced with the PlayStation Network but not the Xbox network (even if logged into a Microsoft account).

Any player's achievement progress can be accessed from the Profile screen, both in-game and in the Xbox app, although privacy settings may restrict profile visibility to friends or only the player themself.

They are independent of one another, allowing players to get them in any order. Once earned, they cannot be reset.

Achievements grant the player Xbox gamerscore on all platforms except PlayStation, totaling 2,980.

Some achievements also give rewards, which include emotes and Character Creator items. They can be unlocked only by completing their respective achievement, but are carried over to all platforms, even those where the player has not completed the achievement.

Unobtainability
thumb|The pop up informing the player that certain settings disable achievements in the Create new world screen
There are some conditions that permanently disable the ability to earn achievements in a world if it is saved with one or more of the following settings. Even if disabled later, achievements can never be earned again on that world.

Creative mode is enabled.
The world was created as a flat world.
External behavior packs are activated.
Cheats are enabled.
The world creator has set people who joined to have operator permissions (cheats on).
Experiments are enabled.
The world was created using the demo mode world.
The world was made from a Marketplace template (not custom).
The world was made from an Editor project.
Additionally, achievements cannot be earned or viewed in Minecraft Preview or the beta version.

List of achievements
Note that the achievements are categorized as they are shown in-game using the default sorting. Trivia
"Fruit on the Loom" is a reference to the American clothing company, Fruit of the Loom
"The Lie" is a reference to "the cake is a lie", a memetic line from Portal.
"Cow Tipper" references to the supposed practice of cow tipping.
"MOAR Tools" references the meme "MOAR".
"Rainbow Collection" references the song "Rainbow Connection" by Kermit the Frog.
"Tie Dye Outfit" refers to the fashion trend of tie-dyeing.
"Beam Me Up" is a quote associated with Star Trek, but never actually used in the series.
"So I Got That Going for Me" references a famous line from the movie Caddyshack.
"Sneak 100" is a reference to two different things: The achievement's icon references Solid Snake, while the name references a popular format of memes involving the various skills in The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim.
"Let It Go!" references the song of the same name from Frozen.
"Moskstraumen" references a system of whirpools in Norway that are among the most powerful on Earth.
"Do a Barrel Roll!" references an iconic line from Star Fox.
"Whatever Floats Your Goat" is a pun on the idiom "whatever floats your boat", meaning "whatever works for you".
"Wax On, Wax Off" is a reference to a recurring line in the 1984 film The Karate Kid.
Regardless of the Minecraft version, new achievements still exist on older versions.
