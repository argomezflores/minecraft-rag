TITLE: Potion of Caring
CATEGORY: Potions
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion_of_Caring

A potion of Caring is a joke potion exclusive to Java Edition 15w14a that provides Caring when used. It replaces the data value of both potions of Slowness and potions of Poison.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Spider Eye + Awkward Potion Revision 1 → Potion of Caring (Poison).

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Caring (Poison) → Splash Potion of Caring (Poison).

Usage
Effect
Caring makes mobs move toward the nearest mob as if it were to attack it.
