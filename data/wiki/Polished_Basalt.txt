Polished basalt is the polished version of basalt and can be found as part of ancient cities and bastion remnants.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished basalt drops as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined by any other tool, it drops nothing.

Natural generation
Polished basalt can be found naturally as part of ancient cities and bastion remnants.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Basalt, Basalt, Basalt, Basalt arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Basalt,4.

Stonecutting

Usage
Polished basalt's only use is as building material. Like logs and quartz pillars, polished basalt blocks point perpendicular to whatever block face they are placed on.
