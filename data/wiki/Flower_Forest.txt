TITLE: Flower Forest
CATEGORY: Overworld biomes
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Flower_Forest
---
A flower forest is a variant of the forest filled with a much larger variation and frequency of flowers. Rabbits spawn here instead of wolves and bee nests generate much more often.

Generation
Flower forests generate at moderate temperatures, very low humidities, and are confined to areas of negative weirdness. This means that they are rarer than regular forests and will never border them.

They most commonly border plains and meadow biomes. Flower forests may border sunflower plains biomes, separated by a river much of the time. They may also occasionally border swamps, savannas, snowy slopes, jagged peaks, and windswept hills.

Description
Flower forests have both oak and birch trees. They generate with a wide array of flowers, along with bee nests on 5% of trees.

Abandoned camps sometimes generate in flower forests.

Differences to regular forests
There are some key differences between regular forests and flower forests.

Flower forests have fewer trees and less grass. Instead, flower forests include a diverse variety of flowers. Leaf litter and bushes are completely absent.
