An awkward potion is a potion that provides no effects. It is used as the basis for brewing most other potions.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Nether Wart + Water Bottle → Awkward Potion.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Awkward Potion → Awkward Splash Potion.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Awkward Splash Potion → Awkward Lingering Potion.

Usage
Base potions
Awkward potion is a base potion. It has no effect when the player drinks it, but is necessary as the basis for other potions.

Lingering potion
, a lingering awkward potion acts similar to the lingering water bottle, being able to extinguish burning entities and damage endermen, blazes, and snow golems, but without the ability to put out fires.

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Awkward Lingering Potion, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Tipped Arrow, 8.
