Trading
Wandering traders have a chance to trade 3 light blue dyes for an emerald.

Mob loot
, wandering traders have a chance of 25% (increased by 1% per level of Looting) to drop 3 light blue dyes when killed while showing the item as trade offer, if the player holds an emerald.
