Mob conversion is a game mechanic where under specific circumstances certain mobs can convert to mobs of a different type, or to a different variant of the same mob.

Mobs which split into several upon death
When a slime or magma cube splits into several smaller ones upon death, the smaller ones inherit their progenitor's data as if the event were a mob conversion, with the exception of the following fields:
