TITLE: Firework Star
CATEGORY: Items
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Firework_Star
---
A firework star is a crafting ingredient used to create explosive firework rockets, which deal damage based on the number of stars added. Firework stars can be crafted using various combinations of dyes and other items, which affect the appearance, colors, and visual effects of the resulting explosions.

Obtaining
In Survival mode, firework stars are obtainable only through crafting. In Creative mode, the generic version or all 16 color variants can be found in the Creative inventory.

Crafting

Additionally,  only, a single unit of bone meal, lapis lazuli, cocoa beans, or ink sacs can be used to set the base color of a firework star. They cannot be mixed with any other ingredient (whether color or effect ingredients), nor used to add a "fade to color" effect.

Usage
The only usage of firework stars is to create firework rockets. Firework stars crafted with rockets trigger damaging explosions after their duration ends, including when used to propel the player while using elytra.
