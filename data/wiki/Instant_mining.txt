Instant mining, or insta-mining, is a game mechanic in which 1 block can be broken per game tick without the delay of  of a second (6 ticks). Blocks that break instantly without using tools do not decrease durability if a tool is used. Below are listed the tool tier and the Efficiency and Haste levels necessary to instant-mine a block.

All blocks have the hardness value of 0, hence 0×30 = 0, and 1 is greater than 0, meaning that all blocks can be broken instantly regardless of enchantments, status effects, and breaking damage penalties.
