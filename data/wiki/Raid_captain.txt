A raid captain or illager captain is the occurrence of an illager that spawns wearing an ominous banner on its head. It is usually a pillager or vindicator, or rarely  an evoker. They are the only renewable source of both ominous banners and ominous bottles, which they drop when killed.

Spawning
Raid captains spawn in mostly the same conditions and occurrences as a regular illager, with a few exceptions.

, a pillager raid captain can be spawned using the  command, and a vindicator raid captain (which is unused in-game) can be spawned using the  command.

, pillagers, vindicators and evokers spawned from spawn eggs, monster spawners or trial spawners also have a small chance to spawn as a raid captain.

Patrols

One pillager raid captain always spawns as part of a patrol, as the leader. These are referred to as “patrol captains” to avoid confusion.

Raids

, one raid captain spawns in each wave during a raid. They usually spawn within a group of pillagers, and sometimes spawns riding a ravager. When its ominous banner is dropped, other illagers rush and attempt to pick it up to become the new raid captain.

, raid captains are unused and do not spawn naturally during raids, but the code for them is present.

Pillager outposts

Pillager raid captains regularly spawn in a pillager outpost. Every pillager spawned has a small chance to be a raid captain.

, only pillagers and passive mobs may spawn within a pillager outpost spawn area.  Pillagers may spawn on any valid opaque block as long as the block light level is 8 or lower.

, raid captains can only spawn first, meaning if there is any regular pillager left nearby, raid captain will never spawn.

Behavior
Raid captains usually act the same as if they were a normal illager, but other illagers follow behind them (except for outpost captain), and usually the captain takes the lead ahead of the other illagers. Once a raid captain is killed by a player, an ominous bottle is dropped that if drank, grant Bad Omen, which can transform into Raid Omen in a village to start a raid, or into Trial Omen when seen by a non-ominous trial spawner, to start an ominous trial.The captain also drops its x16px ominous banner as well, and it can be picked up by another illager for it to become the captain.
