A potion of Invisibility is a potion that provides Invisibility when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Night Vision → Potion of Invisibility.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Invisibility → Potion of Invisibility Extended.

Mob loot
Wandering traders have an 8.5%/25% chance (increased by 1% per level of Looting) of dropping a potion of Invisibility if they die while they are drinking the potion, or holding the potion as a trade offer.

Trading
Wandering traders can sell extended potions of Invisibility for 5 emeralds.
