Deepslate bricks are the brick variant of deepslate.

Obtaining
Breaking
Deepslate bricks can be obtained only by mining them with any pickaxe. When mined using any other tool, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Deepslate bricks generate naturally in ancient cities.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate arranged in the crafting grid → Deepslate Bricks,4.
