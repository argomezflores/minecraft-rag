A bone is an item primarily obtained from skeletons and similar mobs. It can be crafted into bone meal and used to tame wild wolves.

Obtaining
Mob loot

Generated loot

Fishing
Bones can be obtained by fishing, as part of the junk category. The best chance of catching a bone is achieved without the Luck of the Sea enchantment, yielding a 1.1% chance.

Usage
Crafting ingredient
A bone can be crafted into 3 bone meal.

Taming
Using a bone on a wild wolf has a  chance of taming it. Bones cannot tame a hostile wolf or a wolf that has already been tamed. However, bones can be used to attract the attention of already tamed wolves.

Helmet

While a bone cannot be equipped in the head slot in Survival mode, equipping it compulsorily by using commands causes it to appear in the player's mouth.
