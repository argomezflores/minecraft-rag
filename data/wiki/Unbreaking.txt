CATEGORY: Priority

Unbreaking is an enchantment that gives a chance for an item to avoid durability reduction when it is used, effectively increasing the item's durability.

alt=Unbreaking Enchantment Tool Negative Binomial Graph|thumb|200px|Graphs showing the probability of a tool breaking after a number of uses with different levels of this enchantment. Some lower durability materials may last longer than higher durability materials with some enchantment levels.

Obtaining
Unbreaking can be obtained from enchanting tables, drops from mobs that have spawned in a raid, books obtained by fishing, monster room chests, librarian villagers, and non-village structure loot.

Usage
For tools and weapons, there is a 100%÷(level+1) chance that using the item reduces durability, which means that the item has a 50%/66.66……%/75% chance of not using any durability. On average, tools last 2/3/4 times as long.
