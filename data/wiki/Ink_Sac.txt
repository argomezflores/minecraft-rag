An ink sac is an item dropped by a squid upon death. It can be used to craft black dye, dark prismarine, and books and quills.

Obtaining
Mob loot

Generated loot

Fishing
A stack of 10 ink sacs can be obtained as a "junk" item from fishing. Ink sacs are the rarest fishing loot, with a  chance of obtaining them from fishing with an unenchanted fishing rod. The Luck of the Sea enchantment decreases the chances of obtaining ink sacs.

Compound creation
Ink sac can be created from its base elements, using the compound creator.
