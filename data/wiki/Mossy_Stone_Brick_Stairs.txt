Mossy stone brick stairs are a stairs variant of mossy stone bricks that generate in ruined portals and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Mossy stone brick stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Mossy stone brick stairs generate as part of ruined portals in the Overworld.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Mossy Stone Bricks, Mossy Stone Bricks, Mossy Stone Bricks, Mossy Stone Bricks, Mossy Stone Bricks, Mossy Stone Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Mossy Stone Brick Stairs,4.
