A mossy stone brick slab is a decorative slab variant of mossy stone bricks that generates in ruined portals and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Mossy stone brick slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Mossy stone brick slabs generate as part of ruined portals in the Overworld.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Mossy Stone Bricks, Mossy Stone Bricks, Mossy Stone Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Mossy Stone Brick Slab,6.
