Bamboo mosaic stairs are a decorative stairs variant of bamboo mosaics that do not generate naturally and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Bamboo mosaic stairs can be broken with anything, but axes are the fastest.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Bamboo Mosaic, Bamboo Mosaic, Bamboo Mosaic, Bamboo Mosaic, Bamboo Mosaic, Bamboo Mosaic arranged in the crafting grid → Bamboo Mosaic Stairs,4.

Usage

Fuel
Bamboo mosaic stairs can be used as fuel in furnaces, smelting 1.5 items per block.
