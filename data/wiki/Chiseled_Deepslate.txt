Chiseled deepslate is a variant of deepslate.

Obtaining
Breaking
Chiseled deepslate can be obtained only by mining it with a pickaxe. When mined using any other tool, it drops nothing.

Natural generation
Chiseled deepslate generate naturally in ancient cities.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Cobbled Deepslate Slab, Cobbled Deepslate Slab arranged in the crafting grid → Chiseled Deepslate.

Stonecutting

Usage
Chiseled deepslate is used for decoration.

Trivia
In 1.17, chiseled deepslate was spelled "Chiselled Deepslate", a departure from the normal US English spelling.
Chiseled deepslate depicts a generalized face, with the intent to give the player face pareidolia.
