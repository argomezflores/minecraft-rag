Chiseled resin bricks are a variant of resin bricks, crafted from resin brick slabs.

Obtaining
Breaking
Chiseled resin bricks can be mined using a pickaxe. When mined without a pickaxe, it drops nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Resin Brick Slab, Resin Brick Slab arranged in the crafting grid → Chiseled Resin Bricks.

Stonecutting

Usage
Chiseled resin bricks are used for decoration. The block has the face of a creaking engraved on it. See also Easter eggs#Mobs on chiseled blocks.
