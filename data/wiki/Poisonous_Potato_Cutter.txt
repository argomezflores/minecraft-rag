A poisonous potato cutter is a joke block only present in 24w14potato. It functions like a stonecutter, except it works only on poisonous potatoes, cutting them into poisonous potato slices or poisonous potato sticks.

Obtaining
Breaking
A poisonous potato cutter can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, it drops nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Poisonous Potato, Potone, Potone, Potone arranged in the crafting grid → Poisonous Potato Cutter.

Usage
Cutting
The poisonous potato cutter can be used to craft poisonous potatoes into poisonous potato slices and poisonous potato sticks.
