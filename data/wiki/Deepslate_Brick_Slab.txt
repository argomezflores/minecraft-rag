A deepslate brick slab is a decorative slab variant of deepslate bricks that generates in ancient cities and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Deepslate brick slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Deepslate brick slabs generate as part of ancient cities.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Deepslate Brick Slab,6.

Stonecutting

Usage
