Andesite stairs are a decorative stairs variant of andesite used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Andesite stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Andesite, Andesite, Andesite, Andesite, Andesite, Andesite arranged in the crafting grid → Andesite Stairs,4.
