If the player's hunger bar is completely filled and they have left over saturation, this saturation gets drained, healing at a rate of  every second. Just as with the hunger-based regeneration, every  regenerated deducts 1.5 saturation. With the maximum amount of 20 saturation, the player can rapidly regenerate up to .

Peaceful difficulty
When the difficulty is set to Peaceful, the player regenerates at a standard rate of  per second, additionally with  every 4 seconds because the player's hunger bar is filled. Chorus fruit, golden apples, honey bottles, and suspicious stew can be eaten even when the hunger bar is full, increasing the player's regeneration even further. On the other hand, this also causes the player to lose saturation even when changing from other difficulty levels to Peaceful mode.

Tipped arrow
Tipped arrow shots from a bow, crossbow, or dispenser inflict arrow damage and a status effect. Arrows of healing give the same effect as its potion ( for an arrow of healing I, and  for an arrow of healing II). Regeneration I arrows last 5 seconds (restores ), the extended Regeneration I arrows last 0:11 / 0:15 seconds (restores  / ), and arrows of Regeneration II give Regeneration II for 0:02 seconds (restores ). However, arrow damage decreases or sometimes even nullifies the health recovered unless a weak bow shot is used or the player is wearing good armor.

Mob healing
In contrast to the player, most mobs cannot heal by themselves. Nevertheless, there are several ways to heal animals and monsters.

Potions
Most mobs are healed by potions of Healing and Regeneration just like the player.

On the other hand, undead mobs take damage from these potions, and healing them requires a potion of Harming. Potions of Regeneration or Poison don't affect undead mobs at all.

Axolotls may play dead when taking damage underwater, dropping to the ground for 10 seconds while regenerating.

Fox
Foxes can eat foods that inflict Regeneration, such as golden apples, enchanted golden apples, or suspicious stew.

Villager
Villagers gain 10 seconds of regeneration when new trades are unlocked.
Additionally , villagers recover health when waking up from bed every morning.

Witch
Witches use potions of Healing to heal themselves.
Additionally, witches throw splash potions of Healing or Regeneration at illagers during raids.
