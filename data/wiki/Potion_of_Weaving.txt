A potion of Weaving is a potion that provides Weaving when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Cobweb + Awkward Potion → Potion of Weaving.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Weaving → Splash Potion of Weaving.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Weaving → Lingering Potion of Weaving.

Usage
Effect
Potion type Tier Duration Effect Description Potion 1 3:00 Spawns 2–3 cobwebs nearby when killed
Doubles walking speed whilst in cobwebs (from 25% to 50%) Splash potion 1 3:00 Lingering potion 1 0:45

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Lingering Potion of Weaving, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Arrow of Weaving, 8.
