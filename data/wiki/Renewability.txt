TITLE: Renewability
CATEGORY: Gameplay
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Renewability
---
Renewability refers to the possibility of an item or block or entity to be obtained continuously without exploring new areas or having additional players join. There exist 2 pages with an explanation of it:

Renewable resource: Resources that can be renewed in Survival, without the use of cheats or duplication exploits.
Non-renewable resource: Resources that can't be renewed in Survival.
The renewability of an item or block includes the use of farms (including automatic farms) and factors in elements such as technically limited but abundant resources. For example, deepslate is abundant, but not actually renewable, as it cannot be obtained beyond its natural generation.
