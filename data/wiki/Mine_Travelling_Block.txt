The mine travelling block is a block only present in Java Edition 25w14craftmine.

Usage
The mine travelling block is used to send the player in and out of a mine upon being clicked. It displays an ever-growing countdown using the action bar before teleporting the player to the dimension set or back to the Hub.

Obtaining
The mine travelling block cannot be obtained as an item even by using the  command. The block can be placed using commands such as  or , and naturally generates in the hub above a mine crafter and in the mine exit. It cannot be destroyed by tools or explosions.
