A deepslate brick wall is a decorative wall variant of deepslate bricks that generates in ancient cities and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Deepslate brick walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Deepslate brick walls generate naturally within ancient cities.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Deepslate Brick Wall,6.
