A polished cinnabar wall is a decorative wall variant of polished cinnabar.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished cinnabar walls drop as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Cinnabar, Polished Cinnabar, Polished Cinnabar, Polished Cinnabar, Polished Cinnabar, Polished Cinnabar arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Cinnabar Wall,6.
