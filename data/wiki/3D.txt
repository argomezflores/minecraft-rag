3D is a joke item only present in Java Edition 3D Shareware v1.34.

Obtaining
Mob drops
3D is dropped by Nerd Creepers summoned via the cheat code "NEEEERD".

Usage
Eating the 3D item when the hunger bar is not full shows a picture of the developer cast of  at the time. Hovering over it in the inventory says "Tasty!" in constantly changing formatting codes.
