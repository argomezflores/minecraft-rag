A block of coal is a block in Java Edition 2.0. It was later added as an authentic block in 1.6.

Obtaining
Breaking
Blocks of coal can be mined using any pickaxe, in which case it drops itself. If mined without a pickaxe, it drops nothing.

Crafting
Similar to other mineral blocks, it is crafted with 9 coal.

Crafting recipe: Coal, Coal, Coal, Coal, Coal, Coal, Coal, Coal, Coal arranged in the crafting grid → [Block of Coal]Block of Coal (April Fools' joke).

Usage
Blocks of coal can be crafted back into coal but the coal will not be stackable with normal coal.The coal does not stack with other coal due to having a data value of .

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: [Block of Coal]Block of Coal (April Fools' joke) arranged in the crafting grid → Coal.

Fuel
Blocks of coal can be used as fuel in a furnace. One block of coal lasts 800 seconds (16000 ticks), which smelts 80 items.
