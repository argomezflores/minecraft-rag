A potion of Oozing is a potion that provides Oozing when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Slime Block + Awkward Potion → Potion of Oozing.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Oozing → Splash Potion of Oozing.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Oozing → Lingering Potion of Oozing.

Usage
Effect
Potion type Tier Duration Effect Description Potion 1 3:00Spawns two size 2 slimes nearby when killed Splash Potion 1 3:00 Lingering Potion 1 0:45

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Lingering Potion of Oozing, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Arrow of Oozing, 8.
