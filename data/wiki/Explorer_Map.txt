Explorer maps are a group of 11 premade map variants which are marked with the location of various structures.

In the third drop of 2026, four new explorer maps will be added as well as maps for abandoned camps in 8 different biomes.

Obtaining
Explorer maps cannot be found in the Creative inventory.

Buried treasure maps can be found in ocean ruins and shipwrecks.

An abandoned camp map can be found in all abandoned camp common chests, and one of seven types of explorer map can be found in all abandoned camp secret chests.

Usage
thumb|217px|Unexplored and partially explored woodland explorer maps

Locating structures
An explorer map shows a section of terrain that contains a specific structure, marked with a small icon. The structure shown may not be the nearest such structure to the player, the structure depends on when it is graded, where it searches for a structure according to the location of the chest or villager. For loot, the map is generated when the loot is generated (opening the chest, destroying the chest, hopper), for trades, the map is generated when the trade is generated. The structure sought is similar to the command  but with a range of 101×101 if it is generated by loot (maintain the 201×201 range if it is generated by trading), and ignore the structures that have already been found by previous maps, except for the buried treasure map.

An explorer map differs from a normal map in that it shows the area's land-water outline, with an orange striated texture for water and swamps,, specifically, biomes in the  biome tag. and the blank map texture for land.

If the player icon is smaller than it would be on a normal map, that means the player is a great distance away. When the player is less than 1027 blocks away from the map border, the icon returns to the proper size. When the player reaches the map's area of land (512×512), the map fills in like a normal map. Unlike the player icon when inside the map area, the player off-map and off-limits icons do not rotate.

It is possible for multiple maps to point to the same treasure or structure, since explorer maps lead to the closest location (relative to where they were created) of their target type.

Cloning

The parts of the world that have already been explored and mapped are copied, and newly explored areas appear on both instances. In Creative mode, cloned explorer maps can be obtained by pick blocking on the explorer map displayed on item frames (the map needs to be out of the player's inventory when using pick block, or else that map moves into the active hotbar slot).

Item data

An explorer map differs from a map in that its map_color and item_name components are set. Additionally, its map_decorations component includes an entry for its target structure. Its [[Map item format#map_<#>.dat format|map item entry]] is identical to that of a normal map, except that by the time the player sees it, its colors byte array is pre-filled with explorer maps' characteristic land-water boundary lines and water shading.
