Air is a joke item only present in Java Edition 23w13a_or_b.

Obtaining
Crafting
This recipe is unlocked by voting air_blocks.

Crafting recipe: Glass Bottle, Glass Bottle, Glass Bottle, Glass Bottle arranged in the crafting grid → Air (April Fools' joke).

Usage
Air can be eaten by pressing and holding  while it is selected in the hotbar to keep a player alive while on the Moon, a joke dimension added in the same update. It is also used to inflate moon cows, that when fully inflated, spring a rider into the air.
