A bottle o' enchanting is an item that can be thrown to break it and release experience orbs.

Usage
A bottle o' enchanting can be thrown by pressing . On impact, it destroys itself and drops experience orbs worth  (average 7.0) and sends out blue particles. Bottles o' enchanting can be used to increase the player's level for enchanting, using an anvil, or repairing tools and armor enchanted with Mending.

Bottles o' enchanting have an enchantment glint like enchanted items do.
