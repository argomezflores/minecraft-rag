An ant is a joke block only present in Java Edition 20w14∞ that moves around when placed on top of black or white concrete.

Obtaining
An ant cannot be obtained as an item. It can be placed using commands such as  or , and naturally generates in the "" dimension. It cannot be mined or exploded.

Natural generation
A single ant naturally generates in the "" dimension, where it begins moving immediately.

Usage
An ant simulates . If placed on top of black or white concrete, it moves in a deterministic pattern, turning the concrete underneath it into its other variation.

If two ant blocks collide, it merges into a single block. It cannot be pushed by pistons, and can replace bedrock. If the concrete beneath it is broken, it stops moving. If a block is in its way, it either pushes the block or destroys it.
