A potion of Weakness is a potion that provides Weakness when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Water Bottle → Potion of Weakness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Strength;Awkward Potion;Mundane Potion;Thick Potion → Potion of Weakness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Long Mundane Potion → Potion of Weakness Extended.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Weakness → Potion of Weakness Extended.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Weakness Extended → Splash Potion of Weakness Extended.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Weakness Extended → Lingering Potion of Weakness Extended.

Thrown projectile
A witch has a 25% chance of throwing a splash potion of Weakness if a player is 3 or less blocks away and does not already have the Weakness effect. Also, the player's health has to be at  or less, or the player has to have the Poison effect.

The thrown potion cannot be picked up, but it can cause the Weakness effect.

Generated loot

One splash potion of Weakness can be found in the brewing stand inside every igloo basement.

, the cauldron in a swamp hut contains 1 to 2 bottles of a random potion. There is a 10% for it to be a potion of Weakness.

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Lingering Potion of Weakness; Lingering Potion of Weakness Extended, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Arrow of Weakness, 8; Arrow of Weakness Extended, 8.

Curing zombie villagers

A golden apple and a splash potion of Weakness can be used to convert a zombie villager into a regular villager.
