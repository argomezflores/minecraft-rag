A big brain is a block, only present in 24w14potato, used to obtain potatoes of knowledge.

Obtaining
Breaking
A big brain can be broken using anything, but an axe is the fastest. Big brains always drop themselves and a potato of knowledge when mined.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Poisonous Potato, Poisonous Potato, Poisonous Potato, Poisonous Potato, Zombie Head, Poisonous Potato, Poisonous Potato, Poisonous Potato, Poisonous Potato arranged in the crafting grid → Big Brain.

Even though the recipe requires a zombie head, the recipe is unlocked by having a wither skeleton skull in the inventory.

Usage
Potatoes of knowledge
Big brains absorb nearby experience orbs. When broken, a big brain drops a potato of knowledge with a knowledge amount equal to how much experience it absorbed (if it didn't absorb anything, potato of knowledge still drops, with 0 knowledge). The maximum possible amount of experience stored in a single potato of knowledge is 1000 experience points, which takes the player from 0 to 25 levels; if the big brain absorbs this much experience, a potato of knowledge with 1000 experience is dropped by it automatically, without breaking the block.

Piston interactivity
Big brains cannot be pushed or pulled by pistons.

Block data

A big brain has a block entity associated with it that holds additional data about the block.
Block entity data

: The amount of experience stored in this big brain.
: An integer that oscillates between 5 and 1 (inclusive) by decreasing by 1 every tick. Experience orbs can enter this big brain only when delay is 1.
