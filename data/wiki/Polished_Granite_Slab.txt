A polished granite slab is a decorative slab variant of polished granite that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished granite slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Granite, Polished Granite, Polished Granite arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Granite Slab,6.
