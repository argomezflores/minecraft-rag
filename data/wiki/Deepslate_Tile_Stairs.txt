Deepslate tile stairs are a decorative stairs variant of deepslate tiles that generate in ancient cities and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Deepslate tile stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Deepslate tile stairs generate as part of ancient cities.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Deepslate Tiles, Deepslate Tiles, Deepslate Tiles, Deepslate Tiles, Deepslate Tiles, Deepslate Tiles arranged in the crafting grid → Deepslate Tile Stairs,4.
