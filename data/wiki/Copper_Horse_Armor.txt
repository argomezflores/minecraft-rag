Copper horse armor is the fifth-strongest tier of horse armor. Unlike other copper items, it cannot be crafted and is only obtainable from chest loot in various structures.

Obtaining

Usage
Only regular adult horses and zombie horses can wear copper horse armor; baby horses, donkeys, mules, and skeleton horses cannot be equipped with armor.

Equipping
Copper horse armor can be equipped to a horse either by manually placing it in its respective slot () in the horse’s inventory, or by pressing  on the horse while holding it.

The copper horse armor can be removed either by manually removing it from the armor slot or by using shears on the horse.

Protection
When equipped, copper horse armor gives  armor points.

Horse armor protects zombie horses from burning in sunlight.

If a horse dies while equipped with copper horse armor, it drops its normal loot, a saddle (if it had one), and the horse armor.

Durability
Unlike player armor or wolf armor, horse armor does not have durability. This means a single copper horse armor can be used infinitely unless it is destroyed through other means.

Enchanting
Unlike player armor, horse armor cannot be enchanted in Survival mode. However,  Creative mode, horse armor can be used with an anvil to enchant with Thorns, Feather Falling, Protection, Fire Protection, Projectile Protection, Blast Protection, Curse of Binding, and Curse of Vanishing. Other enchantments can be applied, but they have no effect.

Smelting

Smelting recipe: Copper Horse Armor → Copper Nugget, XP: 0,1.
