Expired baked potato bricks are a type of bricks crafted from baked potatoes and poisonous potatoes, only present in 24w14potato.

Obtaining
Breaking

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Baked Potato, Poisonous Potato, Poisonous Potato, Baked Potato arranged in the crafting grid → Expired Baked Potato Bricks.

Natural generation
Expired baked potato bricks naturally generate in colosseums and potato villages.
