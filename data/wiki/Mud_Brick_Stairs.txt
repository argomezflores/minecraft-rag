Mud brick stairs are a decorative stairs variant of mud bricks that generate in trail ruins and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Mud brick stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Mud brick stairs generate as part of trail ruins.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Mud Bricks, Mud Bricks, Mud Bricks, Mud Bricks, Mud Bricks, Mud Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Mud Brick Stairs,4.
