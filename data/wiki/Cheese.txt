TITLE: Cheese
CATEGORY: Blocks
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cheese

Cheese is a joke block and a food that can be eaten by the player. It generates on The Moon as the main ground block, referencing a common joke about the moon being made of cheese. It is only present in the 23w13a_or_b snapshot.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cheese drops itself if it has not been bitten. Once the cheese is bitten, it cannot be recollected, even with the use of Silk Touch.

Mob loot
Upon death, moon cows drop 0-2 cheese. The maximum amount is increased by 1 per level of Looting, for a maximum of 5 cheese blocks with Looting III.

Milk bucket
If the minecraft:instacheese vote is active, using a milk bucket on the ground places cheese, similarly to powder snow. It cannot be picked up into the bucket again.

Usage
Similarly to cakes, cheese can be eaten when placed in the world. However, unlike cakes, the cheese block is divided into 8 smaller cubes. Clicking on one of them eats only the selected part, unlike cakes, where slices cannot be chosen.

Eating cheese restores both hunger and oxygen, allowing the player to survive on the Moon.
