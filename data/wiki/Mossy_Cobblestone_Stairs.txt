Mossy cobblestone stairs are a decorative stairs variant of mossy cobblestone that generate in pillager outposts and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Mossy cobblestone stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Mossy cobblestone stairs generate as part of pillager outposts.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Mossy Cobblestone, Mossy Cobblestone, Mossy Cobblestone, Mossy Cobblestone, Mossy Cobblestone, Mossy Cobblestone arranged in the crafting grid → Mossy Cobblestone Stairs,4.
