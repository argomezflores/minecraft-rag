The dragon's breath is a brewing item that is used solely to brew lingering potions.

Obtaining
Harvesting
Dragon's breath can be obtained by  empty glass bottles on the ender dragon's breath attack while perched, or on the purple effect clouds caused by dragon fireballs.

Usage
Brewing ingredient
Dragon's breath may be added to any splash potion to upgrade it to a lingering potion, which can be used to create a lasting area effect cloud, or to craft tipped arrows.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Matching Splash Potion → Matching Lingering Potion.

With two or more dragon's breath in the brewing stand, after the brewing process, a glass bottle drops as an entity.
