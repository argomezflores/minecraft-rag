A potion of Poison is a potion that provides Poison when used.

Obtaining
Generated loot

In Bedrock Edition, the cauldron in a swamp hut has a 25% chance of containing a potion of Poison.

Brewing

Brewing recipe: Spider Eye + Awkward Potion → Potion of Poison.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Poison → Potion of Poison Extended.

Brewing recipe: Glowstone Dust + Potion of Poison → Potion of Poison Enhanced.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Poison Extended → Splash Potion of Poison Extended.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Poison Extended → Lingering Potion of Poison Extended.

Base potion
Potion of Poison is a base for brewing potions of Harming.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Poison → Potion of Harming.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Poison Enhanced → Potion of Harming Enhanced.

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Lingering Potion of Poison; Lingering Potion of Poison Enhanced; Lingering Potion of Poison Extended, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Arrow of Poison, 8; Arrow of Poison Enhanced, 8; Arrow of Poison Extended, 8.
