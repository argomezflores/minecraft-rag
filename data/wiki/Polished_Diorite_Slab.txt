A polished diorite slab is a decorative slab variant of polished diorite that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished diorite slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Diorite, Polished Diorite, Polished Diorite arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Diorite Slab,6.
