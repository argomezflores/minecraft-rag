A polished blackstone wall is a decorative wall variant of polished blackstone that generates in nether ruined portals and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished blackstone walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Polished blackstone walls generate as part of Nether Ruined Portals.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Blackstone Wall,6.
