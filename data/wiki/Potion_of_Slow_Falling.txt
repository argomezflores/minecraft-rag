A potion of Slow Falling is a potion that provides Slow Falling when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Phantom Membrane + Awkward Potion → Potion of Slow Falling.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Slow Falling → Potion of Slow Falling Extended.

Usage
Effect
Potion type Tier Duration Effect Description Potion 1 Regular: 1:30Extended: 4:00Fall speed and acceleration is significantly reduced and fall damage is completely negated. Splash potion 1 Regular: 1:30Extended: 4:00 Lingering potion 1 Regular: 0:22Extended: 1:00

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Lingering Potion of Slow Falling; Lingering Potion of Slow Falling Extended, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Arrow of Slow Falling, 8; Arrow of Slow Falling Extended, 8.
