Potato oil is a joke potion that provides It's very slippery when used.

Obtaining
Potato oil can be obtained via commands, and is also available in the Creative inventory.

Brewing
While the base potato oil cannot be obtained via brewing, it can still be brewed into a splash potion or a lingering potion.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potato Oil (Potion) → Splash Potato Oil.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potato Oil → Lingering Potato Oil.
