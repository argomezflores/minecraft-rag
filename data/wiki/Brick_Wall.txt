A brick wall is a decorative wall variant of bricks that generates in trail ruins and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Brick walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Brick walls can generate in trail ruins.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Bricks, Bricks, Bricks, Bricks, Bricks, Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Brick Wall,6.
