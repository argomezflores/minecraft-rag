A polished tuff slab is a decorative slab variant of polished tuff that generates in trial chambers and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished tuff slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Polished tuff slabs generate in trial chambers, next to all ominous vaults.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Tuff Slab,6.
