A block of stripped bamboo is a variant of the block of bamboo made by  an axe on a block of bamboo.

Obtaining
Breaking
Blocks of stripped bamboo can be broken by hand, but using an axe speeds up the process.

Stripping
an axe on a block of bamboo turns it into a block of stripped bamboo.

Usage
Crafting ingredient

Fuel
Blocks of stripped bamboo can be used as fuel in furnaces, smelting 1.5 items per block.

Unlike logs, blocks of bamboo cannot be smelted into charcoal.
