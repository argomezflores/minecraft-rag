A cut red sandstone slab is a decorative slab variant of cut red sandstone that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cut red sandstone slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Cut Red Sandstone, Cut Red Sandstone, Cut Red Sandstone arranged in the crafting grid → Cut Red Sandstone Slab,6.
