A polished deepslate slab is a decorative slab variant of polished deepslate that generates in ancient cities and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished deepslate slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Polished deepslate slabs generate as part of ancient cities.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Deepslate Slab,6.

Stonecutting

Usage
