TITLE: Blaze Powder
CATEGORY: Items
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze_Powder
---
Blaze powder is an item crafted from a blaze rod, which is dropped by a blaze when killed. Its main uses are to fuel brewing stands, brew potions of Strength, and make eyes of ender, which lead the player to strongholds and open End portals, which take the player to the End.

Obtaining
Crafting

Usage
Crafting ingredient

Brewing stand fuel
In addition to being a brewing ingredient, blaze powder is needed to fuel a brewing stand. Each piece of blaze powder can brew up to 20 batches of potions, thus up to 60 potions in total.

Brewing ingredient

Brewing recipe: Blaze Powder + Water Bottle → Mundane Potion.
