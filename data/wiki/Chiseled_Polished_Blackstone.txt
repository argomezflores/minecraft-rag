Chiseled polished blackstone is the chiseled variation of polished blackstone. It has a piglin nose figure.

Obtaining
Breaking
Chiseled polished blackstone needs to be broken using a pickaxe, otherwise the block drops nothing.

Natural generation
Chiseled polished blackstone can generate in bastion remnants and nether ruined portals.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Blackstone Slab, Polished Blackstone Slab arranged in the crafting grid → Chiseled Polished Blackstone.

Stonecutting

Usage
Chiseled polished blackstone is a decorative block. It has a piglin's snout engraved on it. See also Easter eggs#Mobs on chiseled blocks.
