End stone brick stairs are a decorative stairs variant of End stone bricks that do not generate naturally and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
End stone brick stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: End Stone Bricks, End Stone Bricks, End Stone Bricks, End Stone Bricks, End Stone Bricks, End Stone Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → End Stone Brick Stairs,4.
