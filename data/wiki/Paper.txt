Paper is an item crafted from sugar cane, required for crafting books, maps, cartography tables, firework rockets, name tags, and banner patterns.

Obtaining
Generated loot

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Sugar Cane, Sugar Cane, Sugar Cane arranged in the crafting grid → Paper,3.

Villager gifts
, cartographer villagers may give paper to players with the Hero of the Village effect.

Usage
Crafting ingredient

Cartography table usage
Paper can be used to zoom out a map by one level in a cartography table. This method of zooming out map is 8 times more efficient than using a crafting table. , it can also be used to create an empty map or an empty locator map. This is also cheaper than the equivalent crafting recipes.
