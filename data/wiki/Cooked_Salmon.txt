Cooked salmon is a food item obtained by cooking raw salmon.

Mob loot

Generated loot

Cooking
Raw salmon can be cooked in a furnace, smoker, or campfire. Each piece of cooked salmon removed from a furnace output slot gives 0.35 experience (22.4 experience per stack).

Smelting recipe: Raw Salmon → Cooked Salmon, XP: 0,35.

Food

To eat cooked salmon, press and hold  while it is selected in the hotbar. Eating one restores  and 9.6 saturation, the same as cooked mutton.

Wolves
Cooked salmon can be fed to wolves only if they are not at full health, healing them by . However, , unlike other wolf food, cooked salmon cannot be used to speed up the growth of baby wolves nor used to breed them and can only be fed to a wolf that is not at full health.
