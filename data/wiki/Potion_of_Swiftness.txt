A potion of Swiftness is a potion that provides Speed when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Sugar + Awkward Potion → Potion of Swiftness.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Swiftness → Potion of Swiftness Extended.

Brewing recipe: Glowstone Dust + Potion of Swiftness → Potion of Swiftness Enhanced.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Swiftness Extended → Splash Potion of Swiftness Extended.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Swiftness Extended → Lingering Potion of Swiftness Extended.

Mob loot
A witch has an 8.5% chance to drop a potion of Swiftness if it dies while drinking the potion.

Generated loot
, the cauldron in a swamp hut contains a random potion, with a 25% chance for it to be Swiftness.

Base potion
Potion of Swiftness is a base for brewing potions of Slowness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Swiftness → Potion of Slowness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Swiftness Extended → Potion of Slowness Extended.
