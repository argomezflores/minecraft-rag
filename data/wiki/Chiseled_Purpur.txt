Chiseled purpur is an unobtainable block in the purpur family, which looks identical to standard purpur blocks.

Breaking
Chiseled purpur must be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, it drops nothing.

Usage
Chiseled purpur can be used as a construction material much like regular purpur blocks.
