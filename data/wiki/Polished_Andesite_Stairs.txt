Polished andesite stairs are a decorative stairs variant of polished andesite used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished andesite stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Andesite, Polished Andesite, Polished Andesite, Polished Andesite, Polished Andesite, Polished Andesite arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Andesite Stairs,4.
