A cinnabar brick wall is a decorative wall variant of cinnabar bricks.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cinnabar brick walls drop as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Cinnabar Bricks, Cinnabar Bricks, Cinnabar Bricks, Cinnabar Bricks, Cinnabar Bricks, Cinnabar Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Cinnabar Brick Wall,6.
