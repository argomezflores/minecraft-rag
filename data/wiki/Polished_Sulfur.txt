Polished sulfur is a decorative polished variant of sulfur.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished sulfur drops as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Sulfur, Sulfur, Sulfur, Sulfur arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Sulfur,4.
