A potion of Strength is a potion that provides Strength when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Blaze Powder + Awkward Potion → Potion of Strength.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Strength → Potion of Strength Extended.

Brewing recipe: Glowstone Dust + Potion of Strength → Potion of Strength Enhanced.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Strength Extended → Splash Potion of Strength Extended.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Strength Extended → Lingering Potion of Strength Extended.

Generated loot
Each trial chambers supply chest can generate with 2 to 10 potions of Strength in it.

Base potion
The potion of Strength is a base for brewing potions of Weakness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Strength → Potion of Weakness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Strength Extended → Potion of Weakness Extended.

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Lingering Potion of Strength; Lingering Potion of Strength Enhanced; Lingering Potion of Strength Extended, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Arrow of Strength, 8; Arrow of Strength Enhanced, 8; Arrow of Strength Extended, 8.
