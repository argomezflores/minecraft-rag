A potion of Night Vision is a potion that provides Night Vision when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Golden Carrot + Awkward Potion → Potion of Night Vision.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Night Vision → Potion of Night Vision Extended.

Generated loot

Usage
Effect
Potions of Night Vision are used by pressing and holding  with the potion bottle in hand, as with all consumable items. When consumed, they give the player Night Vision and return a glass bottle.

Drinking a potion while already under the effects of the same potion does not add onto the effect's duration, but simply resets it.
Potion type Tier Duration Effect Description Potion 1 Regular: 3:00Extended: 8:00Enhances the player's ability to see in darkness and underwater. Splash potion 1 Regular: 3:00Extended: 8:00 Lingering potion 1 Regular: 0:45Extended: 2:00

Base potion
Potion of Night Vision is a base for brewing potions of Invisibility.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Night Vision → Potion of Invisibility.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Night Vision Extended → Potion of Invisibility Extended.

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Lingering Potion of Night Vision; Lingering Potion of Night Vision Extended, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Arrow of Night Vision, 8; Arrow of Night Vision Extended, 8.
