TITLE: Feather
CATEGORY: Items
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Feather

Feathers are items dropped by chickens and parrots, and can be found in loot chests of certain structures. Tamed cats may give players feathers as morning gifts.

Obtaining
Mob loot

A fox sometimes spawns holding a feather, which always drops upon death. Alternatively, the player can drop a food item, causing the fox to drop the feather in favor of the food.

Generated loot

Cat gifts

Tamed cats have a 70% chance of giving the player a "morning gift", and the gift has a 16.13% chance to be a feather.

Usage
Crafting ingredient

Trading

Helmet

Although a feather cannot be equipped in the head slot in Survival mode, equipping it using commands causes it to appear to be rotated upright unlike other items.
