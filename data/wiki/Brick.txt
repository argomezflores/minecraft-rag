A brick is an item mainly obtained by smelting clay balls used to craft brick blocks, flower pots, and decorated pots.

Obtaining
Mining
When breaking a decorated pot with a tool without Silk Touch on the main hand, the decorated pot can drop 0-4 brick(s) depending on the material that it was made of.

Generated loot

Smelting
A brick can be obtained by smelting a clay ball.

Smelting recipe: Clay Ball → Brick, XP: 0,3.

Trading

Usage
Bricks can be used to craft brick blocks, flower pots, and decorated pots.
