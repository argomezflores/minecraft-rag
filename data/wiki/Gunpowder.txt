Gunpowder is an item used for explosion-related recipes, and as an ingredient in potions to create splash potions.

Obtaining
Mob loot

, wandering traders have a chance of 25% (increased by 1% per level of Looting) to drop 4 gunpowder when killed while showing the item as trade offer, if the player holds an emerald.

Creeper farms, ghast farms, and witch farms are all efficient ways of obtaining gunpowder automatically or semi-automatically. However, in Bedrock Edition, creeper farms stop working after a few hours.

Generated loot

Trading
Wandering traders can occasionally sell 4 gunpowder for 1 emerald.

Usage
Crafting ingredient

Brewing ingredient

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Matching Potion → Matching Splash Potion.
