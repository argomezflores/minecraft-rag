A potato fence gate is a potato variant of a fence gate, crafted from the respective potato planks. It is only present in 24w14potato.

Obtaining
Breaking
Potato fence gate can be broken with anything, but axes are faster.

Natural generation
Potato fence gates generate naturally as part of potato villages.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Stick, Potato Planks, Stick, Stick, Potato Planks, Stick arranged in the crafting grid → Potato Fence Gate.
