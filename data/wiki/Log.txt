TITLE: Log
CATEGORY: Blocks
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Log
---
A log or stem is a naturally occurring block found in trees or huge fungi, primarily used as a building block and to create planks, a versatile crafting ingredient. There are currently nine types of logs: oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, and pale oak; and two types of stems: crimson and warped. The upcoming third drop of 2026 introduces poplar as the tenth log type.

Obtaining
Breaking
Logs and stems can be broken by hand, but using an axe is faster. Logs and stems drop themselves when broken with any tool.

Natural generation
All types of logs generate naturally as part of trees, although fallen trees can only contain oak, spruce, birch, jungle, or poplar. Azalea trees are generated with oak logs. Additionally, one jungle log generates for each jungle bush. Stems generate naturally as part of huge fungi.

Cherry logs generate as part of abandoned camps in cherry groves.

Pale oak logs generate as part of abandoned camps in pale gardens.

Poplar logs generate as part of abandoned camps in dappled forests.

Generated loot

Trading
Wandering traders can sell 8 logs for one emerald.

Mob loot
, wandering traders have a chance of 25% (increased by 1% per level of Looting) to drop 8 available logs when killed while showing the item as trade offer, if the player holds an emerald.

Post-generation
Logs and stems
Trees can be grown using saplings or azalea, and huge fungi can be grown using small Nether fungi.

Smelting ingredient

Smelting recipe: {Any Log} → Charcoal, XP: 0,15.

Stems cannot be smelted into charcoal.

Fuel
Logs, but not stems, can be used as a fuel in furnaces, smelting 1.5 items per block.

Stripping
an axe on a log or stem strips it, turning it into a stripped log or a stripped stem, which act the same as regular logs or stems.

Cocoa beans
Cocoa beans can be placed on the side of jungle logs to grow a new cocoa pod.
