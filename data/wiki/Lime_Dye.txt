Lime dye is one of the sixteen available dyes.

Obtaining
Crafting

Smelting

Smelting recipe: Sea Pickle → Lime Dye, XP: 0,1.

Smelting recipe: Sea Pickle → Lime Dye, XP: 0,2.

Trading
Wandering traders have a chance to trade 3 lime dyes for an emerald.

Mob loot
, wandering traders have a chance of 25% (increased by 1% per level of Looting) to drop 3 lime dyes when killed while showing the item as trade offer, if the player holds an emerald.
