A potato fence is a potato variant of a fence, crafted from the respective potato planks. It is only present in 24w14potato.

Obtaining
Breaking
Potato fences can be broken with anything, but axes are faster.

Natural generation
Potato fences generate naturally as part of potato villages, potato mineshafts, as well as in arboretum and fields biomes.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Potato Planks, Stick, Potato Planks, Potato Planks, Stick, Potato Planks arranged in the crafting grid → Potato Fence,3.
