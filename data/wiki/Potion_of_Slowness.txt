A potion of Slowness is a potion that provides Slowness when used.

Obtaining
, the cauldron in a swamp hut has a 10% chance of containing a potion of Slowness.

Brewing

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Swiftness → Potion of Slowness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Leaping → Potion of Slowness.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Slowness → Potion of Slowness Extended.

Brewing recipe: Glowstone Dust + Potion of Slowness → Potion of Slowness Enhanced.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Slowness Extended → Splash Potion of Slowness Extended.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Slowness Extended → Lingering Potion of Slowness Extended.

Usage
Effect
Slowness reduces movement speed. Movement includes walking, sprinting, sneaking, crawling, and swimming. If the a player or mob is riding another mob, Slowness only reduces the speed of the ridden mob if that mob has the effect. The speed of the rider does not matter.
