A diorite slab is a decorative slab variant of diorite that generates in snowy villages and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Diorite slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural Generation
Diorate slabs generate as part of snowy villages.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Diorite, Diorite, Diorite arranged in the crafting grid → Diorite Slab,6.

Stonecutting
