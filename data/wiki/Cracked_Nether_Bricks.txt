Cracked Nether bricks are a decorative variant of Nether bricks, obtained by smelting Nether bricks.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cracked Nether bricks can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Smelting

Smelting recipe: Nether Bricks → Cracked Nether Bricks, XP: 0.1.

Usage
Cracked nether bricks are a decorative block.
