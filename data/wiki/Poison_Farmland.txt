Poison farmland is a joke block found throughout the Potato dimension in 24w14potato. It mostly acts as normal farmland.

Obtaining
Breaking
When mined (even with a Silk Touch tool), poison farmland drops terre de pomme. It can be obtained as an item only by using commands, via the Creative inventory or the  control in Creative mode.

Natural generation
Poison farmland occurs naturally in the Potato dimension: it covers a large portion of the fields biome (where only potatoes are growing on it), as well as generating in potato villages (where potatoes, wheat seeds, beetroot seeds and carrots can be found growing on it).

Post-generation
A player can create a block of poison farmland by  a hoe on terre de pomme, peelgrass block, corrupted peelgrass block or poison path.

Usage
Plants can grow on poison farmland.
