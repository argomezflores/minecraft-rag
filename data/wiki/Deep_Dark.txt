TITLE: Deep Dark
CATEGORY: Overworld biomes
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Deep_Dark
---
The deep dark is a cave biome that generates deep underground in the Overworld, most often beneath mountainous areas. Covered in sculk, no hostile mobs spawn here except for the warden which appears after activating sculk shriekers.

Generation
The deep dark generates deep underground, typically staying within the deepslate layer, though it can occasionally reach into the stone layer when mountains above reach high altitudes.

Because the deep dark tends to generate beneath regions with low erosion values, it is most frequently found beneath mountain biomes, including frozen peaks, jagged peaks, stony peaks, groves, and snowy slopes. Plateau biomes like cherry groves, meadows, savanna plateaus, and pale gardens also have relatively low erosion values, allowing the deep dark to sometimes generate beneath these biomes. The deep dark will almost never generate under rivers, oceans, windswept biomes, swamps, or mangrove swamps.

Description
The deep dark is covered in large patches of sculk blocks surrounded by sculk veins, which include sculk-related blocks such as sculk shriekers, sculk sensors, and sculk catalysts.

The biome is dimly lit; there is almost no glow lichen, with the only natural light sources being sculk catalysts and lava. No regular mob spawning occurs in this biome, but other mechanics are unaffected: monster rooms can still spawn mobs, and in very specific cases phantoms, skeleton horse traps, pillager patrols and wandering traders along with their trader llamas can also still spawn here. Furthermore, the warden may appear if four shriekers are triggered, giving the player the Darkness effect.

The deep dark is the only biome where ancient cities can generate. Other structures and features that can be found in the deep dark include monster rooms, strongholds, and amethyst geodes. Mineshafts and trial chambers never generate in a deep dark biome, although they may extend into one.

While certain ores that are exposed to air blocks have a chance to not generate, sculk blocks are considered a solid block during generation, causing many ores to be revealed under it.

The deep dark is the only Overworld biome that lava lakes can't appear in, even if deep dark generates above Y=0.

If a deep dark were to generate at the surface, it would appear like plains with grassy terrain and sculk patches. , the flower gradients also match plains, which allows players to grow flowers like tulips using bone meal underground.

Trivia
The deep dark was originally "going to feel like an underwater biome, but with the water missing" along with "lots of floating jellyfish in the air and ghostly looking blocks." White, orange, teal, and lime green were some of the color themes meant for the deep dark. In these versions, the only source of light would have been bioluminescent blocks.
In a few images shown by Kingbdogz during the 1.19 development cycle showed fossils generating in this biome covered in sculk veins which was confirmed to have been something they were "just testing out", and it never made it into the game.
