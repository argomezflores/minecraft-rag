Bleach is an item only available in Bedrock or Education Edition used to dye blocks white. It can work as a replacement for white dye. It is also known as a compound, as it is made using a lab table.

Obtaining
Lab table
Bleach is obtainable in the Creative inventory, or using , or lab table.
