TITLE: Potion of Healing
CATEGORY: Potions

A potion of Healing is a potion that provides Instant Health when used.

The potion effect heals all non-undead mobs and entities, for undead mobs it deals damage.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Glistering Melon Slice + Awkward Potion → Potion of Healing.

Brewing recipe: Glowstone Dust + Potion of Healing → Potion of Healing Enhanced.

Mob loot
A witch has an 8.5% chance to drop a potion of Healing if it dies while drinking the potion.

Generated loot
the cauldron in a swamp hut has a 25% chance to contain a potion of Healing.

Two Instant Health II potions can be found in the brewing stand on End ships in End cities.

Undead mobs

The effect of potions of Healing are reversed on undead mobs. It deals damage instead of healing them.

Base potion
Potion of Healing is a base for brewing potions of Harming.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Healing → Potion of Harming.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Healing Enhanced → Potion of Harming Enhanced.

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Lingering Potion of Healing; Lingering Potion of Healing Enhanced, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Arrow of Healing, 8; Arrow of Healing Enhanced, 8.
