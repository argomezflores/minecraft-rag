A thick potion is a potion that provides no effects. , it can be used to brew potions of Weakness.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Glowstone Dust + Water Bottle → Thick Potion.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Thick Potion → Thick Splash Potion.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Thick Splash Potion → Thick Lingering Potion.

Usage
Base potion
:

Only usable as an alternative base for potion of Weakness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Thick Potion → Potion of Weakness.

Lingering potion
, a lingering thick potion acts similar to the lingering water bottle, being able to extinguish burning entities and damage endermen, blazes, and snow golems, but without the ability to put out fires.

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Thick Lingering Potion, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Tipped Arrow, 8.
