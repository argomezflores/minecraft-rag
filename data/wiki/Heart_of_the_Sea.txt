A heart of the sea is an item found only in buried treasure chests. It, alongside nautilus shells, is required to craft a conduit.

Obtaining
A heart of the sea is only obtained from a buried treasure. The location is marked with a noticeable red X on a buried treasure map, which is found in ocean ruins and shipwrecks.

Trivia
Before the heart of the sea was added to , there was an entry found in the buried treasure chest loot table named nautilus_core, which was later revealed to be the heart of the sea. Despite the name change, the heart of the sea continues to be referred to as the nautilus_core in most game files.
