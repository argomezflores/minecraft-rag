A diamond sword is a variant of sword that is crafted from diamonds. It is the second-highest tier of sword, dealing more damage than an iron sword and having higher durability. It can be upgraded into a netherite sword using a netherite ingot and netherite upgrade at a smithing table.

Obtaining
Generated loot

Crafting
A diamond sword is crafted using 1 stick and 2 diamonds.

Crafting recipe: Diamond, Diamond, Stick arranged in the crafting grid → Diamond Sword.

Usage
Breaking

A sword can also be used to destroy certain blocks 50% quicker, sometimes much quicker than with fists. Using a sword to destroy any block that doesn't break instantly by hand decreases its durability by 2; this includes bamboo, despite that the sword is the fastest tool for breaking it.

If a sword is enchanted with Silk Touch, either using Creative or commands, cobwebs the sword destroys drop the cobwebs themselves instead of the usual string. This is due to the sword being classified as the proper tool for cobwebs.

Creative mode
In Creative mode, swords are unable to break blocks. However, they can still break placed entities such as minecarts, paintings, item frames, and armor stands.

Repairing
Combining

Anvil repair

A diamond sword can be repaired in an anvil by adding diamonds, with each repair diamond restoring 25% the sword's maximum durability, rounded down. Two diamond swords can also be combined in an anvil. Both methods preserve the diamond sword's enchantments.

Upgrading
Like other diamond items, a diamond sword can be upgraded to a netherite sword, increasing its durability and attack damage, as well as granting resistance to fire and lava when dropped as an item.
