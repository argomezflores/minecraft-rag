Leather is an item used to craft bundles, books, saddles, armor, and other items.

Obtaining
Mob loot

A fox sometimes spawns holding leather, which always drops upon death. Alternatively, a player can drop a food item, causing the fox to drop the leather in favor of that food.

Generated loot

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Rabbit Hide, Rabbit Hide, Rabbit Hide, Rabbit Hide arranged in the crafting grid → Leather.

Fishing
Leather can be obtained as a "junk" item from fishing, with a 1% default chance of getting it.

Bartering
Adult piglins may barter 2-4 leather with a chance of ~8.53% () when given a gold ingot.

Villager gifts
, leatherworker villagers throw leather at players under the Hero of the Village effect.
