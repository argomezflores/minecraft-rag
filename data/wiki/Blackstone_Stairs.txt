Blackstone stairs are a decorative stairs variant of blackstone that generate in bastion remnants and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Blackstone stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Blackstone stairs generate as part of bastion remnants.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Blackstone, Blackstone, Blackstone, Blackstone, Blackstone, Blackstone arranged in the crafting grid → Blackstone Stairs,4.
