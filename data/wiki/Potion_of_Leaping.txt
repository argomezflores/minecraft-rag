A potion of Leaping is a potion that provides Jump Boost when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Rabbit's Foot + Awkward Potion → Potion of Leaping.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Leaping → Potion of Leaping Extended.

Brewing recipe: Glowstone Dust + Potion of Leaping → Potion of Leaping Enhanced.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Leaping Extended → Splash Potion of Leaping Extended.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Leaping Extended → Lingering Potion of Leaping Extended.

Base potion
Potion of Leaping is a base for brewing potions of Slowness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Leaping → Potion of Slowness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Leaping Extended → Potion of Slowness Extended.

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Lingering Potion of Leaping; Lingering Potion of Leaping Enhanced; Lingering Potion of Leaping Extended, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Arrow of Leaping, 8; Arrow of Leaping Enhanced, 8; Arrow of Leaping Extended, 8.
