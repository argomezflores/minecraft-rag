A potion of Fire Resistance is a potion that provides Fire Resistance when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Magma Cream + Awkward Potion → Potion of Fire Resistance.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Fire Resistance → Potion of Fire Resistance Extended.

Mob loot
A witch has an 8.5% chance to drop a potion of Fire Resistance if it dies while drinking the potion.

Generated loot

The cauldron in a swamp hut has a 5% chance to contain a potion of Fire Resistance.

Bartering
When given a gold ingot, piglins have a  (~1.71%) chance to barter a potion of Fire Resistance, and an equal chance to barter a splash potion of Fire Resistance.
