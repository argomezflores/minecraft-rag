Hash browns are a food item obtained by frying potato peels and restores 2 hunger when consumed, only present in 24w14potato.

Obtaining
potato peels of any color on a frying table cooks them into hash browns. This process takes 30 seconds and can be done only on the frying table. Corrupted potato peels can't be fried.

Usage
To eat hash browns, press and hold  while it is selected in the hotbar. Eating one restores  hunger and 2.4 saturation.
