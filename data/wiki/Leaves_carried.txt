leaves_carriedhttps://www.minecraftforum.net/forums/minecraft-pocket-edition/mcpe-discussion/1968560-my-report-on-3-strange-new-blocks?comment=8 was a block that was used as the item model for leaves.

Obtaining
leaves_carried is known to be obtainable only through the use of inventory editors and other external tools.

Breaking
The speed at which leaves_carried breaks is not currently known. They drop regular leaves when broken with shears.

Usage

Appearance
In its base state, leaves_carried highly resembles actual leaves. There are, however, differences: the main difference is that it appears darker than normal leaves, which is due to them using a tint, despite the texture they use already being colored, rather than the grayscale that tinted blocks usually use. Specifically, this tint is the color .

However, higher metadata versions of leaves_carried behave unexpectedly. Perhaps most interesting is metadata 1, which corresponds to spruce leaves, which uses a crafting table's side textures rather than anything relevant to leaves. This could be explainable through examination of terrain.png, however, this is yet to be done.

Functional differences from leaves
Despite very closely resembling leaves visually, leaves_carried do not decay.

Whether they drop saplings when broken without shears is unknown. If broken with shears they drop regular leaves.
