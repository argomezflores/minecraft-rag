Follow Me is a joke item only present in 26w14a.

Obtaining
It is automatically obtained and appears in the hotbar when the player starts up a world. It also appears in the hotbar after the player dies.

Usage
Right-clicking living blocks with this item will add them to a group, and left-clicking will leash all the blocks in the group.
