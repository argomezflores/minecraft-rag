A cobblestone wall is a decorative wall variant of cobblestone that generates in Overworld structures and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cobblestone walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Cobblestone walls generate in woodland mansions. They also generate in pillager outpost towers, abandoned camps, and in some houses in plains, taiga, and snowy tundra villages. They can also generate in snowy taiga villages .

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Cobblestone, Cobblestone, Cobblestone, Cobblestone, Cobblestone, Cobblestone arranged in the crafting grid → Cobblestone Wall,6.
