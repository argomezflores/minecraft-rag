A polished tuff wall is a decorative wall variant of polished tuff that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished tuff walls drop as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Tuff Wall,6.
