Cracked polished blackstone bricks are the cracked variant of polished blackstone bricks.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cracked polished blackstone bricks need to be broken using a pickaxe, otherwise the block drops nothing.

Natural generation
Cracked polished blackstone bricks generate as part of bastion remnants and ruined portals in the Nether.

Smelting

Smelting recipe: Polished Blackstone Bricks → Cracked Polished Blackstone Bricks, XP: 0.1.

Usage
Cracked polished blackstone bricks are a decorative block.
