A diorite wall is a decorative wall variant of diorite that generates in snowy tundra villages and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Diorite walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Diorite walls may generate in snowy tundra villages.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Diorite, Diorite, Diorite, Diorite, Diorite, Diorite arranged in the crafting grid → Diorite Wall,6.
