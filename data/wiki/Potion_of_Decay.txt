A potion of Decay is a potion that provides the Wither effect when used.

Obtaining
Potions of Decay‌ can be obtained via commands, and are also available in the Creative inventory, obtained as a tipped arrow rarely in fletcher trades in chances of 0.1%[1]

Brewing
While the base potion of Decay cannot be obtained via brewing, it can still be brewed into a splash potion or a lingering potion.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Decay → Splash Potion of Decay.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Decay → Lingering Potion of Decay.
