A cobbled deepslate wall is a decorative wall variant of cobbled deepslate that generates in ancient cities and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cobbled deepslate walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Cobbled deepslate walls generate naturally within ancient cities.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Cobbled Deepslate, Cobbled Deepslate, Cobbled Deepslate, Cobbled Deepslate, Cobbled Deepslate, Cobbled Deepslate arranged in the crafting grid → Cobbled Deepslate Wall,6.
