Usage
Food

To eat a golden carrot, press and hold  while it is selected in the hotbar. Eating one restores  and 14.4 saturation points. In Java Edition, due to its high saturation, a golden carrot restores the most health via saturation boost of any stackable food item.

Animal food
Golden carrots are used to tame, breed, lead, grow, and heal horses, donkeys, and mules, and to breed, lead, and grow rabbits. , due to a bug, skeleton horses (in their hostile state; being ridden by skeletons) can also be fed golden carrots.
