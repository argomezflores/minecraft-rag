A potion of Regeneration is a potion that provides Regeneration when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Ghast Tear + Awkward Potion → Potion of Regeneration.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Regeneration → Potion of Regeneration Extended.

Brewing recipe: Glowstone Dust + Potion of Regeneration → Potion of Regeneration Enhanced.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Regeneration Extended → Splash Potion of Regeneration Extended.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Regeneration Extended → Lingering Potion of Regeneration Extended.

Mob loot
A witch has an 8.5% chance to drop a potion of Regeneration if it dies while drinking the potion.

Generated loot
Each trial chambers supply chest can generate with 2 potions of Regeneration  in it. Each ancient city

supply chest can generate with 1-3 potions of regeneration in it.

Usage
Potions of Renegeration allow the player to quickly regenerate health. Splash and lingering potions of Regeneration work on most mobs and heal those mobs just as much as they heal players.
