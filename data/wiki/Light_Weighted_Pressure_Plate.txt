TITLE: Light Weighted Pressure Plate
CATEGORY: Blocks

A light weighted pressure plate is a type of pressure plate that produces a redstone signal while one or more entities stand on it. Depending on how many entities are detected, the outputted signal strength will range from 1 to 15.

Obtaining
Breaking
A light weighted pressure plate can be mined with anything, but pickaxes are the fastest. A light weighted pressure plate also breaks and drops itself if a piston extends or pushes a block into its location, or if the block under the pressure plate is moved or destroyed.

Generated loot

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Gold Ingot, Gold Ingot arranged in the crafting grid → Light Weighted Pressure Plate.

Activation

Light weighted pressure plates can be activated by most entities (including players, mobs, items, arrows, experience orbs, fishing bobs, etc.) and emit a redstone signal strength that is equal to the amount of entities on top of them, up to a maximum power level of 15. Certain entities, however (such as players in spectator mode and markers), cannot activate them nor keep them activated.

, item frames and glow item frames cannot activate light weighted pressure plates but they can still keep them activated.This behavior can be observed by placing an item frame inside of a light weighted pressure plate and then stepping on it: the pressure plate doesn't revert to its inactive state even after the player steps off.

The signal strength from a light weighted pressure plate does not vary with the type of entity. 15 mobs will produce the same signal strength as 15 different items. Note the light weighted pressure plate counts entities, not the quantity of items, generally leading to one entity count per stack of items. For more information see  item entity behavior.

Distracting piglins
Piglins are attracted towards light weighted pressure plates, run toward any light weighted pressure plates on the ground, and inspect them for 6 to 8 seconds before putting them in their inventory slot. During this time, the piglin does not attack nearby players, even if they are not wearing gold armor.
