A Nether brick wall is a decorative wall variant of Nether bricks that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Nether brick walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Nether Brick Wall,6.
