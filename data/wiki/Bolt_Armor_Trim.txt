TITLE: Bolt Armor Trim
CATEGORY: Items
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bolt_Armor_Trim
---
Bolt armor trim is a type of smithing template used in smithing tables to add a trim to armor. It is consumed when used, but can be duplicated using an existing template, a block of copper or waxed block of copper, and seven diamonds.

Obtaining
Bolt armor trims are found in standard vaults and chests in trial chambers. Once obtained, it can be duplicated using the crafting recipe below.

Cloning

Crafting recipe: Diamond, Bolt Armor Trim, Diamond, Diamond, Block of Copper; Waxed Block of Copper, Diamond, Diamond, Diamond, Diamond arranged in the crafting grid → Bolt Armor Trim Smithing Template,2.

Generated loot

Usage
It is an ingredient in its own recipe, making it possible to craft more after the initial item is found.

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Diamond, Bolt Armor Trim, Diamond, Diamond, Block of Copper; Waxed Block of Copper, Diamond, Diamond, Diamond, Diamond arranged in the crafting grid → Bolt Armor Trim Smithing Template,2.

Smithing ingredient

Using the smithing table, the bolt armor trim can be applied to any armor piece, using any trim material.
