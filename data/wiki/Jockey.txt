A jockey refers to a naturally-spawned mob riding another mob. The mob on the top often controls the mob on the bottom , but sometimes does not control it .

Most jockeys are named based on the mob being ridden. The exceptions to the "jockey" naming scheme are the skeleton horseman and zombie horseman.

Most jockeys spawn in similar conditions to their respective mobs, except for baby zombie (variant) riders , which can mount any mob.

Mounting

, 15% of naturally spawned baby zombies, baby husks, and gurgles can mount other mobs when spawned. This includes baby zombies summoned by monster spawners and spawn eggs. Such a mob can be summoned with the minecraft:as_baby_jockey spawn event.
