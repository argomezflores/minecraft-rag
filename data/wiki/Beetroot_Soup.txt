Beetroot soup is an unstackable food item.

Obtaining
Chest Loot

Crafting

Usage

Beetroot soup can be eaten to restore  hunger points and 7.2 saturation, the same as cooked chicken and mushroom stew. After eating, the empty bowl remains, similarly to mushroom stew, suspicious stew, and rabbit stew.
