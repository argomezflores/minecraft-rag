TITLE: Warm Ocean Ruins
CATEGORY: Generated structures
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warm_Ocean_Ruins
---
Warm ocean ruins are variants of ocean ruins. They are underwater structures primarily composed of sandstone. They are found in ocean biomes and rarely on beaches, and are inhabited by drowned and sometimes zombie nautiluses alongside them. They contain chests and suspicious sand that contain a variety of loot, most notably buried treasure maps, exclusive pottery sherds, nautilus armor, emeralds, and sniffer eggs.

Generation
Warm ocean ruins generate in warm, lukewarm, and deep lukewarm ocean biomes, and are composed of sandstone and stone materials as well as having some blocks of suspicious sand within them.

Ocean ruins may at times be at least partially buried, including with their airspaces. During generation, all air blocks and structure blocks act as structure voids. This means that their associated suspicious blocks are also located under layers of usual sand, gravel, and even stone. Suspicious sand randomly replaces sand respectively during generation, and are not part of the structure definition.

Although most ocean ruins generate underwater, they sometimes generate on land, bordering a beach or another seaside biome.

30% of ocean ruins generate as large ocean ruins; the other 70% generate as small ruins. If the ocean ruin is large, there is a 90% chance for 4-8 (inclusive) small ocean ruins to generate around it in a cluster. Large warm ocean ruins generate with a structure integrity of 0.9 and small warm ocean ruins generate with a structure integrity of 0.8. Reduced structure integrity may also remove drowned and zombie nautiluses from the structures.

Warm ocean ruins can be located with warm ocean ruins explorer maps, which are found in secret chests in abandoned camps.

Structure

The shape and size of warm ocean ruins varies greatly, and they often generate together in groups to create ruined villages.

All but two of the structures contain a loot chest with loot inside. The chest contains loot for specifically either a small or large sized ocean ruin structure and may be buried under blocks such as sand.

Warm ocean ruins are primarily composed of sand, sandstone, and suspicious sand, and they also contain polished granite, polished diorite, sea lanterns, magma blocks, gravel, terracotta, sandstone stairs, or a loot chest.

Natural warm ocean ruins generate following the rules above and may not exactly match the stored structures.

The structures below are located in the folder client.jar/data/minecraft/structures/underwater_ruin. The following is a complete table of the structures with their filenames, descriptions and what they consist of.

Each  indicates either a chest with random loot or the spawn location of a drowned/zombie nautilus mob.

Mobs
link=Drowned  Drowned Zombie Nautilus

Upon world generation, many of the structures spawn with drowned (usually one or two, though a few structures have three or four). These drowned are persistent, and do not despawn. Adult drowned wielding tridents may also also spawn riding a zombie nautilus as a zombie nautilus jockey.

Zombie nautiluses spawned from ruins are also persistent. They even spawn on Peaceful difficulty despite being monsters, because they count as part of the structure and ignore conventional spawning mechanics..
