An End stone brick slab is a decorative slab variant of End stone bricks that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
End stone brick slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: End Stone Bricks, End Stone Bricks, End Stone Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → End Stone Brick Slab,6.
