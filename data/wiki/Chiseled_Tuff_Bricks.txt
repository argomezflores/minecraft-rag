TITLE: Chiseled Tuff Bricks
CATEGORY: Blocks
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chiseled_Tuff_Bricks
---
Chiseled tuff bricks are a variant of tuff bricks, featuring a large brick in the center of the block, with geometric designs above and below.

Obtaining
Breaking
Chiseled tuff bricks drop as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Chiseled tuff bricks generate naturally in trial chambers.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Tuff Brick Slab, Tuff Brick Slab arranged in the crafting grid → Chiseled Tuff Bricks.

Stonecutting

Usage
Chiseled tuff bricks are a decorative block.
