A deepslate tile wall is a decorative wall variant of deepslate tiles that generates in ancient cities and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Deepslate tile walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Deepslate tile walls generate naturally within ancient cities.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Deepslate Tiles, Deepslate Tiles, Deepslate Tiles, Deepslate Tiles, Deepslate Tiles, Deepslate Tiles arranged in the crafting grid → Deepslate Tile Wall,6.
