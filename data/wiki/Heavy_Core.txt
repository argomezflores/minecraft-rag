A heavy core is a rare block that can only be obtained from ominous vaults found in trial chambers. It can be combined with a breeze rod to craft a mace.

Obtaining
Generated loot

Breaking
A heavy core can be mined with any tool or by hand, but a pickaxe is the most effective. It always drops itself when mined.

Usage
Crafting ingredient

Piston interactivity
, just like glazed terracotta, heavy cores can be pushed by pistons but can't be pulled by sticky pistons. They also don't stick to slime blocks and honey blocks. , they can be both pushed and pulled by pistons. They also stick to slime blocks and honey blocks.
