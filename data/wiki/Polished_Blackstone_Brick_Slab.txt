A polished blackstone brick slab is a decorative slab variant of polished blackstone bricks that generates in ruined portals and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished blackstone brick slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Polished blackstone brick slabs generate as part of ruined portals in the Nether.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Blackstone Brick Slab,6.
