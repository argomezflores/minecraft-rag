Poisonous potato oil is a joke potion that provides It's very slippery and Poison when used.

Obtaining
Poisonous potato oil can be obtained via commands, and is also available in the Creative inventory.

Brewing
While the base poisonous potato oil cannot be obtained via brewing, it can still be brewed into a splash potion or a lingering potion.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Poisonous Potato Oil (Potion) → Splash Poisonous Potato Oil.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Poisonous Potato Oil → Lingering Poisonous Potato Oil.
