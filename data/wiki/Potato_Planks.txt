Potato planks are a joke block, only present in 24w14potato.

Obtaining
Breaking

Natural generation
Potato planks generate as part of potato villages and potato mineshafts. Additionally, they generate in arboretum biome on "park lanes".

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Potato Stem arranged in the crafting grid → Potato Planks,4.
