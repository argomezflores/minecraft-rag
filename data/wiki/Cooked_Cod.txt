Cooked cod is a food item obtained by cooking raw cod.

Obtaining
Mob loot

Generated loot

Cooking
Raw cod can be cooked in a furnace, smoker, or campfire. Each piece of cooked cod removed from a furnace output slot gives 0.35 experience (22.4 experience per stack).

Smelting recipe: Raw Cod → Cooked Cod, XP: 0,35.

Usage

Food

To eat cooked cod, press and hold  while it is selected in the hotbar. Eating one restores  hunger and 6 hunger saturation.

Wolves
Cooked cod can be used to feed a wolf not at full health, healing by . However, , unlike other wolf food, cooked cod cannot be used to speed up the growth of baby wolves nor used to breed them and can only be fed to a wolf that is not at full health.
