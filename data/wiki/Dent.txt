A dent is a joke item only present in 24w14potato, used to craft a trident.

Obtaining
Mob loot
Dents have a 100% chance to be dropped by plaguewhale slabs upon death.

Usage
Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Dent, Dent, Stick, Dent, Stick arranged in the crafting grid → Trident.
