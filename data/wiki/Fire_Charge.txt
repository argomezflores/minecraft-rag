A fire charge is an item that can be  as a single-use version of a flint and steel or shot as a small fireball from a dispenser.

Obtaining
Despite firing small fireballs, blazes do not drop fire charges.

Generated loot

Crafting

Bartering
When given a gold ingot, piglins have a ~8.53% () chance to barter a single fire charge.

Natural occurrence

During ominous trials, each ominous trial spawner has a  chance to dispense a fire charge on top of either a player or a mob it spawns. The fire charge is directly dispensed into the world and cannot be obtained in the inventory.

Usage
Dispenser
200px|thumb|Dispensed small fireball

Fire charges can be used as ammunition in dispensers. When a fire charge is fired from a dispenser, it shoots a small fireball. However, a player cannot simply  a fire charge while holding it to shoot a small fireball.

Fire

When used, a fire charge instantly places a fire, similar to flint and steel. But, unlike flint and steel, the fire charge is consumed in the process. It can prime TNT and light nether portals, campfires, candles, cakes with candles, and creepers.
