Polished granite stairs are a decorative stairs variant of polished granite used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished granite stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, the block will drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Granite, Polished Granite, Polished Granite, Polished Granite, Polished Granite, Polished Granite arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Granite Stairs,4.
