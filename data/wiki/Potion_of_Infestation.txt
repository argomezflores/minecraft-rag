A potion of Infestation is a potion that provides the Infested effect when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Stone + Awkward Potion → Potion of Infestation.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of Infestation → Splash Potion of Infestation.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of Infestation → Lingering Potion of Infestation.

Usage
