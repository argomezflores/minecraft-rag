A chicken jockey is the rare appearance of a baby zombie, baby husk, or a gurgle (or baby zombie villager, or baby zombified piglin Not  due to  and .) riding a chicken. , baby zombies and variants can also mount other common mobs.

Spawning
, chicken jockeys spawn directly in the world, like all other jockeys. , baby zombies and certain variants do not spawn directly but instead mount other nearby mobs.

Direct spawning

Each baby zombie, baby zombie villager, baby husk, gurgle, or baby zombified piglin that spawns naturally has a 5% chance to check for an existing chicken within an approximately 10×6×10 (L*H*W) box centered on the baby's spawn location and spawn riding one of those chickens if there are any. If it fails that 5% chance, there is an additional 5% chance of the baby spawning mounted on a new adult chicken.

Therefore, in a chicken-free environment, each baby zombie has a 4.75% chance of spawning directly as a chicken jockey. If chickens are present, this chance increases to 9.75%.

Taking into consideration the fact that each type of zombie has a 5% chance of spawning as a baby, each spawned zombie has a 0.2375% chance of directly spawning as a chicken jockey in a chicken-free environment. If chickens are present, the chance increases to 0.4875%.

Chickens that are mounted by zombies despawn naturally, cannot lay eggs, and drop  experience instead of the usual . If a jockey spawns riding a new chicken, the variant of the chicken depends on the biome it spawns in. If a jockey spawns riding an existing chicken, the chicken keeps its NBT data (variant, health, custom name, age, etc), but its IsChickenJockey tag is set to 1.

Chicken jockeys can sometimes spawn with equipment. Zombie, zombie villager and husk chicken jockeys may spawn holding an iron sword, iron spear, or iron shovel, and wearing armor. Drowned chicken jockeys may spawn holding a trident, fishing rod, or nautilus shell. Zombified piglin chicken jockeys always spawn holding a golden sword or golden spear.

Although chickens take damage from standing on magma blocks, zombified piglin chicken jockeys still spawn on them, killing the chicken and leaving just the baby zombified piglin.

Zombified piglin chicken jockeys do not become hostile if the chicken is attacked or killed.

Due to the biomes the riders spawn in, husk and zombified piglin chicken jockeys can only spawn with a warm chicken, unless they spawn riding an existing chicken. If a zombified piglin chicken jockey spawns from a Nether portal block, the variant of the chicken depends on the biome the portal block is located in.

Drops
The baby zombie (or variant) and the ridden mob each drop the same items they normally would. If the baby zombie or baby husk is killed by a player or tamed wolf while riding a chicken, it also drops the Lava Chicken music disc. Note that the jockey must be a zombie, not a gurgle or a baby zombie villager, husk,, or zombified piglin, and it must be riding a chicken.

Behavior

The jockey’s behavior is controlled by the rider.

Chicken jockeys fall slowly and do not take fall damage, like normal chickens.
The ridden mob can run through 1 block high gaps if its hitbox allows it. The rider suffocates unless the above block is transparent.
The rider can pick up/equip items if capable.
Chickens (unless spawned with commands without the  tag) do not lay eggs. However, they can still breed with other chickens, and the offspring do lay eggs. , if a baby zombie or a variant mounts a normal chicken, the chicken stops laying eggs. If the rider is killed, the chicken starts laying eggs again.
Zombie riders (both the riders and ridden mobs) despawn like regular hostile mobs.
, the chicken acts like a regular passive mob again if the rider is killed, but still does not lay eggs and may despawn.
Only the ridden mob needs a name tag to prevent both the ridden mob and rider from despawning.
