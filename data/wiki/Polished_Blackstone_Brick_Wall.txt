A polished blackstone brick wall is a decorative wall variant of polished blackstone bricks that generates in ruined portals and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished blackstone brick walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Polished blackstone brick walls generate naturally within ruined portals that generate in the Nether.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Blackstone Brick Wall,6.
