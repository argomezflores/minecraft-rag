TITLE: Fermented Spider Eye
CATEGORY: Items
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fermented_Spider_Eye
---
A fermented spider eye is an item used to corrupt the effects of certain potions via brewing, and it is required to brew potions of Weakness.

Obtaining
Crafting

Usage
Fermented spider eyes can be used to brew potions with negative effects, as well as potions of Invisibility. As a general rule, it inverts the effect of the potion (e.g. Speed to Slowness, Instant Health to Instant Damage).

Brewing ingredient

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Water Bottle → Potion of Weakness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Strength;Awkward Potion;Mundane Potion;Thick Potion → Potion of Weakness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Poison;Potion of Healing → Potion of Harming.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Swiftness;Potion of Leaping → Potion of Slowness.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Night Vision → Potion of Invisibility.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Long Mundane Potion → Potion of Weakness Extended.

Trading
Wandering traders occasionally purchase a fermented spider eye for 3 emeralds.
