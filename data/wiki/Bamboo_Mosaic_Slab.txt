A bamboo mosaic slab is a decorative slab variant of bamboo mosaics that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Bamboo mosaic slabs can be broken with anything, but axes are the fastest.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Bamboo Mosaic, Bamboo Mosaic, Bamboo Mosaic arranged in the crafting grid → Bamboo Mosaic Slab,6.

Usage

Fuel
Bamboo mosaic slabs can be used as fuel in furnaces, smelting 0.75 items per slab , and 1.5 items per slab .

Sounds
, when a bamboo mosaic slab is combined into a double slab, the block's step sound is played.
