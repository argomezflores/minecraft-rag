"Pigstep" refers to both a song composed by Lena Raine, and a music disc that plays the song when inserted in a jukebox. The music disc is obtained from bastion remnants.

Track description

File:Pigstep.ogg

The title "Pigstep" is a  of "piglin" and "dubstep".

The song is an intense, somewhat hip-hop-style beat beginning with a repeating cello-like tune and dubstep-style drop, along with more mellow parts reminiscent of a radio. The song was featured in A Minecraft Movie.

The album release of "Pigstep" includes two versions: the mono mix used in the game and a stereo mix with more usage of multi-directional sound.

Usage
Like all music discs,  the "Pigstep" music disc on a jukebox causes the jukebox to play the song.

If a redstone comparator is reading a jukebox playing "Pigstep", it emits a redstone signal of 13.
