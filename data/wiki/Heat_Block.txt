TITLE: Heat Block
CATEGORY: Blocks
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Heat_Block
---
Heat block is a block that can melt snow and ice like a torch, but it does not give off light.

Obtaining
Breaking
The suitable tool to break heat block is pickaxe. Heat block needs to be broken using a pickaxe, otherwise the breaking time will increase and the block drops nothing.

Heat block drops itself when it's destroyed.

Lab table
Result Materials neededHeat Block Iron, Water, Charcoal, Salt

Usage
Heat blocks melt snow layers and ice within 2 blocks (taxicab distance). It is unique in that it is the only block that melts objects without giving off light.

Trivia
The ingredients of the heat block corresponds to the contents of hand warmers. A hand warmer generates heat due to the same electrochemistry that rusts iron in seawater, but with powdered iron the effect becomes more appreciable.
