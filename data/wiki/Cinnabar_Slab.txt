A cinnabar slab is a decorative slab variant of cinnabar used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cinnabar slabs drop as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Cinnabar, Cinnabar, Cinnabar arranged in the crafting grid → Cinnabar Slab,6.
