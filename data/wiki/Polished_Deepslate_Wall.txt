A polished deepslate wall is a decorative wall variant of polished deepslate that generates in ancient cities and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished deepslate walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Polished deepslate walls generate naturally within ancient cities.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Deepslate Wall,6.
