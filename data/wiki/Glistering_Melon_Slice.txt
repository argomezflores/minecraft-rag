A glistering melon slice is an inedible item used as a brewing ingredient for potions of Healing.

Obtaining
Generated loot

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Gold Nugget, Gold Nugget, Gold Nugget, Gold Nugget, Melon Slice, Gold Nugget, Gold Nugget, Gold Nugget, Gold Nugget arranged in the crafting grid → Glistering Melon Slice.

Trading

Usage
Despite being crafted with a melon slice, a glistering melon slice cannot be eaten, unlike a golden apple or golden carrot.

Brewing ingredient

Brewing recipe: Glistering Melon Slice + Water Bottle → Mundane Potion.

Piglins
Piglins run toward any glistering melon slice item on the ground and inspect it for 6 seconds  or 8 seconds  before putting it in their inventory.
