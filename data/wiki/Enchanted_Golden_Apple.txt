An enchanted golden apple is an uncraftable food item that, upon consumption, grants much more powerful effects than a regular golden apple. Despite its name and enchantment glint, enchanted golden apples do not actually have any enchantments.

Obtaining

Generated loot

Usage
Food

To eat an enchanted golden apple, press and hold  while it is selected in the hotbar. It restores  points of hunger and 9.6 saturation.

The enchanted golden apple provides:
Absorption IV for 2 minutes (providing )
Regeneration level II for 20 seconds  (long enough to heal ) or 30 seconds  (long enough to heal )
Fire Resistance I for 5 minutes
Resistance I (20% reduced damage from all sources except the void and starvation) for 5 minutes

Horses
Enchanted golden apples can be used for improving the chances of taming a horse by 10%, for breeding and leading horses, and for speeding up the growth of baby horses by 4 minutes. , due to a bug, skeleton horses (in their hostile state; being ridden by skeletons) can also be fed enchanted golden apples.

Crafting ingredient

Piglins
Piglins run toward any enchanted golden apple item on the ground and inspect it for 6 seconds  or 8 seconds  before putting it in their inventory. However, piglins do not eat enchanted golden apples (or any food).

Trivia
The damage tag of an enchanted golden apple obtained  is 1, although setting it to 0 makes no difference.
, if a regular golden apple is enchanted using an anvil in Creative mode, it has a glint like an enchanted golden apple, but the effect when consumed remains the same as that of a regular golden apple.
Continuously eating enchanted golden apples allows the player to survive in the void until all of the apples are consumed.
