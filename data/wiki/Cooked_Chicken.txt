Cooked chicken is a food item that is obtained by cooking raw chicken.

Obtaining

Mob loot

Generated loot

Cooking

Smelting recipe: Raw Chicken → Cooked Chicken, XP: 0,35.

Trading

Villager gifts
Butcher villagers may give cooked chicken to players with the Hero of the Village effect.

Usage
Food

To eat cooked chicken, press and hold  while it is selected in the hotbar. Eating one restores  hunger and 7.2 hunger saturation.

Wolves
Cooked chicken can be used to breed and heal tamed wolves, lead them around, and make baby tamed wolves grow up faster by 10% of the remaining time.
