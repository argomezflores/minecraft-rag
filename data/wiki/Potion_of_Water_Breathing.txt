A potion of Water Breathing is a potion that provides Water Breathing when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Pufferfish + Awkward Potion → Potion of Water Breathing.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of Water Breathing → Potion of Water Breathing Extended.

Mob loot
A witch has an 8.5% chance to drop a potion of Water Breathing if it dies while drinking the potion.

Generated loot

, the cauldron in a swamp hut has a 10% chance to contain a potion of Water Breathing.
