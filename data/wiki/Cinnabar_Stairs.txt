Cinnabar stairs are a decorative stairs variant of cinnabar used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cinnabar stairs drop as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Cinnabar, Cinnabar, Cinnabar, Cinnabar, Cinnabar, Cinnabar arranged in the crafting grid → Cinnabar Stairs,4.
