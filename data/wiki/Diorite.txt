Diorite is a block of igneous rock, found in large pockets underground.

Obtaining
Breaking
Diorite can be mined using any pickaxe. If broken without a pickaxe, it drops nothing.

Natural generation

Diorite can generate in the Overworld in the form of ore blobs.

, diorite attempts to generate in two batches in all biomes in the Overworld. The first batch generates 2 times per chunk in blobs of size 0-864, from elevations 0 to 60. The second batch has  chance to generate blobs of size 0-864, from elevation 64 to 128. , diorite can replace stone, andesite, granite, polished andesite, polished diorite, and polished granite. , diorite can replace stone, andesite, granite, tuff, and deepslate.

Diorite generates in weaponsmith houses, tannery houses, and mason houses in snowy villages.

, diorite generates in coral reefs, replacing some blocks of coral crust.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Cobblestone, Nether Quartz, Nether Quartz, Cobblestone arranged in the crafting grid → Diorite,2.

Usage
Diorite has the same blast resistance as stone, meaning it can be used as a substitute for stone when building.
