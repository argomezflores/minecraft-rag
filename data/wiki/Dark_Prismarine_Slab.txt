A dark prismarine slab is a decorative slab variant of dark prismarine that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Dark prismarine slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Dark Prismarine, Dark Prismarine, Dark Prismarine arranged in the crafting grid → Dark Prismarine Slab,6.

Stonecutting

Usage
