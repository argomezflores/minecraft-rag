Granite stairs are a decorative stairs variant of granite that generate in desert villages and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Granite stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Granite stairs generate as part of desert villages.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Granite, Granite, Granite, Granite, Granite, Granite arranged in the crafting grid → Granite Stairs,4.
