A blackstone wall is a decorative wall variant of blackstone that generates in bastion remnants and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Blackstone walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Blackstone walls generate naturally within bastion remnants.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Blackstone, Blackstone, Blackstone, Blackstone, Blackstone, Blackstone arranged in the crafting grid → Blackstone Wall,6.
