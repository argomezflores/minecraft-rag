An andesite wall is a decorative wall variant of andesite that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Andesite walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Andesite, Andesite, Andesite, Andesite, Andesite, Andesite arranged in the crafting grid → Andesite Wall,6.
