A potion of Harming is a potion that provides Instant Damage when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Healing → Potion of Harming.

Brewing recipe: Fermented Spider Eye + Potion of Poison → Potion of Harming.

Brewing recipe: Glowstone Dust + Potion of Harming → Potion of Harming Enhanced.

Usage
Effect
Potion type Tier Duration Effect DescriptionPotion 1Instant Damages . 2 Damages  (doubles with every additional level).Splash Potion 1 Damages . 2 Damages  (doubles with every additional level).Lingering Potion 1 Damages . 2 Damages  (doubles with every additional level).

Crafting ingredient

Crafting recipe: Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Lingering Potion of Harming; Lingering Potion of Harming Enhanced, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow, Arrow arranged in the crafting grid → Arrow of Harming, 8; Arrow of Harming Enhanced, 8.

A potion of Harming heals when used on undead mobs.
