A polished sulfur slab is a decorative slab variant of polished sulfur.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished sulfur slabs drop as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Sulfur, Polished Sulfur, Polished Sulfur arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Sulfur Slab,6.
