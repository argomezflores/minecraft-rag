Apples are food items that rarely drop from certain leaves and can be eaten by the player or crafted into golden apples.

Obtaining
Block loot

Oak and dark oak leaves have a 0.5% () chance of dropping an apple when decayed or broken, but not if burned. Breaking leaves with a tool enchanted with the Fortune enchantment increases the chances of dropping an apple: 0.556% () with Fortune I, 0.625% () with Fortune II, and 0.833% () with Fortune III.

Generated loot

Trading

Usage

To eat an apple, press and hold  while it is selected in the hotbar. Eating one restores  hunger and 2.4 saturation.

Unlike most foods, apples can be eaten with a full hunger bar .

Apples can be used to heal, grow, and increase temper of horses, donkeys, and mules. , due to a bug, skeleton horses (in their hostile state; being ridden by skeletons) can also be fed apples.

Crafting ingredient

Composting
Placing an apple into a composter has a 65% () chance of raising the compost level by 1.
