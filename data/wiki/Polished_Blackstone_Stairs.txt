Polished blackstone stairs are a decorative stairs variant of polished blackstone that generate in ruined portals and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished blackstone stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Polished blackstone stairs generate as part of ruined portals in the Nether.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Blackstone Stairs,4.
