A potato pressure plate is a variant of a pressure plate made of potato planks, only present in 24w14potato.

Obtaining
Breaking

Natural generation
Potato pressure plates can generate naturally as part of potato villages.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Potato Planks, Potato Planks arranged in the crafting grid → Potato Pressure Plate.
