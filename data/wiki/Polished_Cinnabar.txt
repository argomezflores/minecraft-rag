Polished cinnabar is the polished version of cinnabar.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished cinnabar drops as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Cinnabar, Cinnabar, Cinnabar, Cinnabar arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Cinnabar,4.
