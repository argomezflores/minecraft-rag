Polished blackstone brick stairs are a decorative stairs variant of polished blackstone bricks that generate in Nether structures and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished blackstone brick stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Polished blackstone brick stairs generate as part of bastion remnants and ruined portals in the Nether.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks, Polished Blackstone Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Blackstone Brick Stairs,4.
