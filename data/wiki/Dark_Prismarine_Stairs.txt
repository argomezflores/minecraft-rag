Dark prismarine stairs are a decorative stairs variant of dark prismarine that do not generate naturally and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Dark prismarine stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Dark Prismarine, Dark Prismarine, Dark Prismarine, Dark Prismarine, Dark Prismarine, Dark Prismarine arranged in the crafting grid → Dark Prismarine Stairs,4.
