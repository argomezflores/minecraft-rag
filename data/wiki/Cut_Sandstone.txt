Cut sandstone is a variant of sandstone, obtained through crafting and stonecutting.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cut sandstone can be mined with any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, the block drops nothing.

Natural generation
Cut sandstone generates in desert villages, desert pyramids, and warm ocean ruins.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Sandstone, Sandstone, Sandstone, Sandstone arranged in the crafting grid → Cut Sandstone,4.

Stonecutting

Usage
Unlike sand, cut sandstone is never affected by gravity.
