TITLE: Copper Nugget
CATEGORY: Items

Copper nuggets are small pieces of copper that can be obtained by smelting copper tools, weapons, or armor, or by crafting it from a copper ingot. One copper nugget is effectively worth one-ninth of a copper ingot.

Obtaining
Crafting

Smelting

Smelting recipe: Copper Sword; Copper Spear; Any copper tools; Any copper armor; Copper Horse Armor; Copper Nautilus Armor → Copper Nugget.

Durability and enchantments do not affect the ability to smelt copper tools, weapons, or armor into copper nuggets.
