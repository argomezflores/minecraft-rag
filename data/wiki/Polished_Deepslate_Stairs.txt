Polished deepslate stairs are a decorative stairs variant of polished deepslate that generate in ancient cities and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished deepslate stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Polished deepslate stairs generate as part of ancient cities.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate, Polished Deepslate arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Deepslate Stairs,4.
