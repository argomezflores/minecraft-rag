TITLE: Hay Bale
CATEGORY: Blocks
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hay_Bale
---
Hay bales are storage blocks equivalent to nine pieces of wheat. They are used as a crafting ingredient, to feed llamas and all living horse variants, reduce fall damage, and extend campfire smoke.

Obtaining
Breaking
Hay bales can be mined using any tool, but a hoe speeds up the process.

Natural generation
Hay bales often generate as piles (and in some houses, animal pens, farms, or meeting points) scattered around in villages located in plains, and less commonly in savanna and desert villages. They also can generate under some campfires in taiga villages and as a part of targets/scarecrows in pillager outposts.

Hay bales can also generate in abandoned camps.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Wheat, Wheat, Wheat, Wheat, Wheat, Wheat, Wheat, Wheat, Wheat arranged in the crafting grid → Hay Bale.

Usage
Hay bales can be used as compact storage of wheat.

Placement
thumb|right|Seen here alongside logs, bone blocks, quartz pillars, purpur pillars, polished basalt, and barrels, hay bales can be oriented in multiple directions.

Hay bales can be placed pointing in all three spatial dimensions, in the same way as a log is placed. However, there is no "six-sided" variant like that of the wood block.

Crafting ingredient

Food
Although players cannot eat hay bales, hay bales serve as a compact wheat supply for crafting food items during long periods away from other food sources. A full stack of 64 hay bales is equivalent to 192  bread.

Animals
Hay bales can be fed to donkeys, horses, llamas, or mules to heal up to 10 hearts. They are an effective method to heal horses if the player wants to heal them in a short period of time. They also speed up the growth of foals by three minutes and baby llamas by 90 seconds. , due to a bug, skeleton horses (in their hostile state; being ridden by skeletons) can also be fed hay bales.

Breeding
Hay bales can be used both to lead and breed llamas.

Falling
Falling onto a hay bale reduces the fall damage by 80%, meaning whatever falls on a hay bale takes 20% of the normal fall damage.

Below are some example damages a player receives falling on a hay bale (assuming no enchantments and/or status effects that reduce fall damage).

+ Falling on a hay bale examples Fall height  Fall damage 8-12 blocks   13-17 blocks   98-102 blocks   103-107 blocks   118-123 blocks

Thus, it is possible to fall onto a hay bale and survive a 100 block drop (starting with full health) or even a 120 block drop (starting with full health plus 2 Absorption hearts).

Campfires
thumb|A campfire emitting smoke

Placing a hay bale under a campfire increases the height to which its smoke particles can rise from 10 blocks to 25 blocks.

Composting
Placing a hay bale into a composter has an 85% chance of raising the compost level by 1. Composting with hay bales is very inefficient, as the hay bale's chance to be composted is only slightly higher than wheat. For example, 900 wheat could be composted to yield 83 bone meal on average, but if crafted into 100 hay bales, they would only yield 12 bone meal on average.

Trading
Wandering traders occasionally purchase a hay bale for a single emerald.
