A Nether brick slab is a decorative slab variant of Nether bricks that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Nether brick slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Nether Brick Slab,6.

Stonecutting

Usage

Despite their appearance, top and double Nether brick slabs do not qualify as Nether brick blocks for the purposes of Nether fortress mob spawning in .

Crafting ingredient

Sounds
, when a Nether brick slab is combined into a double slab, the block's use sound is played.
