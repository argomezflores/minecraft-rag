Polished diorite stairs are a decorative stairs variant of polished diorite used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished diorite stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Diorite, Polished Diorite, Polished Diorite, Polished Diorite, Polished Diorite, Polished Diorite arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Diorite Stairs,4.
