Deepslate brick stairs are a decorative stairs variant of deepslate bricks that generate in ancient cities and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Deepslate brick stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Deepslate brick stairs generate as part of ancient cities.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks, Deepslate Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Deepslate Brick Stairs,4.
