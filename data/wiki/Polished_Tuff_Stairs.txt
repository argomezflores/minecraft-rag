Polished tuff stairs are a decorative stairs variant of polished tuff that do not generate naturally and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished tuff stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff, Polished Tuff arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Tuff Stairs,4.
