An enchanted golden poisonous potato is a food item, only present in 24w14potato, that grants Absorption, Bad Luck, Luck and Poison when consumed.

Obtaining
Crafting

Crafting recipe: Block of Gold, Block of Gold, Block of Gold, Block of Gold, Poisonous Potato, Block of Gold, Block of Gold, Block of Gold, Block of Gold arranged in the crafting grid → Enchanted Golden Poisonous Potato.

Usage

To eat an enchanted golden poisonous potato, press and hold  while it is selected in the hotbar. It restores  points of hunger and 9.6 hunger saturation.

Unlike most foods, enchanted golden poisonous potatoes can be eaten even if the hunger bar is full.

The enchanted golden poisonous potato provides:
Absorption IV for 2 minutes (providing  points of absorption health)
Poison VIII for 20 seconds (dealing  damage)
Luck and Bad Luck for 5 minutes

Damage from Poison can be reduced by armor with Protection or Resistance effect, resulting in net gain of health points.

Trivia
The crafting recipe is a reference to the old crafting recipe of the enchanted golden apple.
The item texture is raw gold, with the poisonous potato sprouts overlaid on it.
