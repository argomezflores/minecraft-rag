A polished andesite slab is a decorative slab variant of polished andesite that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished andesite slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Andesite, Polished Andesite, Polished Andesite arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Andesite Slab,6.

Stonecutting
