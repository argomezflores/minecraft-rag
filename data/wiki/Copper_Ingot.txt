Copper ingots are metal ingots obtained from smelting raw copper and from killing drowned or copper golems.

Obtaining
Crafting

Crafting recipe: Copper Nugget, Copper Nugget, Copper Nugget, Copper Nugget, Copper Nugget, Copper Nugget, Copper Nugget, Copper Nugget, Copper Nugget arranged in the crafting grid → Copper Ingot.

Smelting
Copper ingots can be obtained by smelting raw copper in a furnace or blast furnace, as well as the ore itself if mined using Silk Touch.

Smelting recipe: Raw Copper → Copper Ingot, XP: 0.7.

Smelting recipe: Copper Ore; Deepslate Copper Ore → Copper Ingot, XP: 0.7.

Mob loot

When killed by a player or a tamed wolf, a drowned has a 11% () chance of dropping a copper ingot. With the Looting enchantment, the chance can be increased to 13% () with Looting I, 15% () with Looting II, and 17% () with Looting III. Copper golems also drop 1-3 copper ingots when killed, which is unaffected by Looting .
