Cinnabar bricks are a decorative brick variant of cinnabar.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cinnabar bricks drop as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Cinnabar, Polished Cinnabar, Polished Cinnabar, Polished Cinnabar arranged in the crafting grid → Cinnabar Bricks,4.
