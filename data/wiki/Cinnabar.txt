Cinnabar is a decorative stone variant block that naturally generates in sulfur caves alongside sulfur, and as part of sulfur springs.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cinnabar drops as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, it drops nothing.

Natural generation
Cinnabar generates throughout sulfur caves and as part of sulfur springs.

Usage
Crafting ingredient
Cinnabar can be crafted into several decorative variants.
