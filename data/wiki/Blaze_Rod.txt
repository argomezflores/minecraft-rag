TITLE: Blaze Rod
CATEGORY: Items
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze_Rod
---
A blaze rod is an item obtained by killing blazes. It is required to craft brewing stands and blaze powder, which is required to fuel brewing stands and craft eyes of ender.

Obtaining
Mob loot

Usage
Crafting ingredient

Fuel
When used in a furnace, a blaze rod lasts 120 seconds (12 items).
