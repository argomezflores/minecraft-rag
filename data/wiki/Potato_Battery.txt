A potato battery is a toggleable block that emits a redstone signal and damages entities above it when activated. It is only present in 24w14potato.

Obtaining
Breaking
A potato battery can be broken using any tool with equal efficiency, and always drops itself.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Iron Ingot, Copper Ingot, Poisonous Potato, Redstone Dust, Poisonous Potato, Poisonous Potato, Poisonous Potato, Poisonous Potato arranged in the crafting grid → Potato Battery.

Usage
Redstone signal
When , a potato battery toggles its state. When active, it emits a redstone signal of 15.

Damage
If an entity is on top of it while it is active, the entity is damaged by  per tick. However, due to damage immunity, the entity actually receives damage only every half a second.
