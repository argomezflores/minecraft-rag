TITLE: Book
CATEGORY: Items
URL: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Book
---
A book is an item that can be used as a crafting material or to store enchantments in a form that can be applied to any valid item using an anvil.

Obtaining
Block loot
Three books are dropped when a bookshelf is broken without using Silk Touch or fire.

Generated loot

Crafting

Grindstones
Disenchanting an enchanted book at a grindstone yields a normal book and a small amount of experience (unless the enchanted book has Curse of Binding or Curse of Vanishing on it).

Villager gifts
, librarian villagers throw books at players under the Hero of the Village effect.

Usage
Crafting ingredient

Chiseled bookshelf
the chiseled bookshelf while having a book in the main hand puts the book inside the chiseled bookshelf.

Enchanting
Books can be made into enchanted books by enchanting them on enchanting tables.
