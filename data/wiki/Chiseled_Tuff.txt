Chiseled tuff is a chiseled variant of tuff.

Obtaining
Breaking
Chiseled tuff drops as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, it drops nothing.

Natural generation
Chiseled tuff generates naturally in trial chambers.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Tuff Slab, Tuff Slab arranged in the crafting grid → Chiseled Tuff.

Stonecutting

Usage
Chiseled tuff is a decorative block. It uses the common wind swirls designs throughout the trial chambers and is similar to the flow banner pattern and wind charge by a breeze. See also Easter eggs#Mobs on chiseled blocks.
