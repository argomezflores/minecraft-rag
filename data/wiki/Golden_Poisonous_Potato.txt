A golden poisonous potato is a food item that grants absorption and poison when consumed, only present in 24w14potato.

Obtaining
Crafting

Crafting recipe: Gold Ingot, Gold Ingot, Gold Ingot, Gold Ingot, Poisonous Potato, Gold Ingot, Gold Ingot, Gold Ingot, Gold Ingot arranged in the crafting grid → Golden Poisonous Potato.

Usage

To eat a golden poisonous potato, press and hold  while it is selected in the hotbar. It restores  points of hunger and 9.6 hunger saturation.

Unlike most foods, golden poisonous potatoes can be eaten even if the hunger bar is full.

The golden poisonous potato provides:
Absorption I for 2 minutes (providing  points of absorption health)
Poison II for 5 seconds (dealing  damage)

Damage from Poison can be reduced by armor with Protection or Resistance effect, resulting in net gain of health points.
