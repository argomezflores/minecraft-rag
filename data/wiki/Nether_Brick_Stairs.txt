Nether brick stairs are a decorative stairs variant of Nether bricks that generate in Nether fortress staircases and are used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Nether brick stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Nether brick stairs generate as part of Nether fortresses.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks, Nether Bricks arranged in the crafting grid → Nether Brick Stairs,4.
