A polished blackstone slab is a decorative slab variant of polished blackstone that generates in ruined portals and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Polished blackstone slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Polished blackstone slabs generate as part of ruined portals in the Nether.

Crafting

Crafting recipe: Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone, Polished Blackstone arranged in the crafting grid → Polished Blackstone Slab,6.
