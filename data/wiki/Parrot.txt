A parrot is a tamable passive mob that spawns in jungle biomes. Parrots imitate sounds of nearby monsters and can perch on the player's shoulders.

Spawning
Parrots naturally spawn in groups of 1–2 in jungles, bamboo jungles, and sparse jungles above logs, leaves, grass blocks, or air.

Unlike most passive mobs, parrots cannot be bred.

Drops
On death

experience orbs are dropped when parrots are killed by a player or a tamed wolf.

Behavior
Parrots fly around idly under normal circumstances, landing frequently to rest. They take interest in any nearby mobs, including those that are hostile to them, and follow them around closely. When attacked, they rapidly fly upward several blocks to flee.

Parrots also flap their wings to swim, slow their falls, and prevent fall damage.

Parrots can be tamed by feeding them wheat seeds, melon seeds, pumpkin seeds, beetroot seeds, torchflower seeds, or pitcher pods. Each item fed has a  chance of successfully taming them. Once tamed,  with a parrot makes it sit down and stand up.

Unless told to sit, a tamed parrot follows the player and teleports if there is a distance of 12 blocks between it and the player.

A death message is displayed to a parrot's owner upon its death.

Because a cookie contains cocoa beans (the main component of chocolate) and chocolate is toxic to real-life parrots, feeding a cookie to a parrot kills it. , the parrot receives  (3.4028 x 1038), damages, and emits Poison particles as it dies. , a cookie inflicts Fatal Poison on the parrot, killing it after a few seconds.

Unlike most animal mobs, parrots cannot breed and baby parrots do not exist.

Sitting on a shoulder
thumb|A player with a parrot on their shoulder.

A tamed parrot on the ground can be made to perch on its player's shoulder by moving through the parrot. On its own, a tamed parrot can also fly to and perch on the player's shoulder, unless it has been told to sit. A player can have one parrot on each shoulder. Parrots always prefer a player's left shoulder first, if it is empty.

, a parrot dismounts its player when the player:
Lands on a higher surface ( block up or higher)
drops off a ledge of higher than  of a block
takes damage
touches water
touches powder snow
is in creative flying mode
starts gliding with an elytra
sleeps on a bed
submerges the player's head in lava (the parrot dismounts and burns even if the player has Fire Resistance)

Parrots on a shoulder always look in the same direction the player's head is looking.

A parrot riding on an entity cannot take any damage (except void and ).

But it may get hurt as soon as it dismounts, as when dismounting a player submerged in lava.

A parrot sitting on the shoulder appears in the inventory interface.

, a parrot sitting on a shoulder prevents the player from entering a nether portal.

If a player uses an ender pearl while parrots are on their shoulder, the parrots teleport with the player.

Imitating sounds
Parrots imitate the idle sounds of nearby monsters and , a few neutral animals as well; they have a detection range of 20 blocks. The sound produced by the parrots is the same sound as the mob being mimicked at a higher pitch, the same pitch as the mob's baby variant. Occasionally, a parrot may imitate sounds of mobs that are not in the area.

, they can also imitate neutral mobs such as endermen, polar bears, wolves and zombified piglins. , they can also imitate piglins, hoglins and piglin brutes.

, parrots attempt to imitate happy ghasts, but without using the corresponding sound event.

Parrots don't imitate the sounds of skeleton horses.

Dancing
Parrots will dance near a jukebox if a music disc is inserted into it. Parrots even have the ability to dance while on a player's shoulder. This is a reference to the Party Parrot meme. They stop dancing when the track ends.

The dancing radius is 3 blocks from the jukebox. If they dance and then fly beyond this radius, they stop dancing. A parrot does not dance if the music disc was inserted prior to it spawning, dismounting, or being within the 3 blocks range.
