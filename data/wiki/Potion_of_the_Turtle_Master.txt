A potion of the Turtle Master is a potion that provides Slowness and Resistance when used.

Obtaining
Brewing

Brewing recipe: Turtle Shell + Awkward Potion → Potion of the Turtle Master.

Brewing recipe: Redstone Dust + Potion of the Turtle Master → Potion of the Turtle Master Extended.

Brewing recipe: Glowstone Dust + Potion of the Turtle Master → Potion of the Turtle Master Enhanced.

Brewing recipe: Gunpowder + Potion of the Turtle Master Extended → Splash Potion of the Turtle Master Extended.

Brewing recipe: Dragon's Breath + Splash Potion of the Turtle Master Extended → Lingering Potion of the Turtle Master Extended.
